## D. Reviewed — no change proposed

- **Policy — archaic word order, not proposed:** "And now will I show you" (11:2), "neither shall he stand" (11:6), "neither…neither" (11:15, 11:17), "Neither shall he regard" (11:37), "shall he carry captive" (11:8). Same as the Matthew caches.
- 11:2 "the realm of Greece": *Yavan*; the note at 8:21 covers it.
- 11:6 "make an agreement" (*la'asot mesharim*): retained; 11:17 "equitable conditions" (*yesharim*): retained.
- 11:8 "molten images" (*nesikhem*): retained.
- 11:15 "well-fortified city" (*'ir mivtsarot*): retained.
- 11:17 "the daughter of women" (*bat ha-nashim*): idiom; retained.
- 11:18 "isles": retained (living word).
- 11:21 "contemptible person" (*nivzeh*): retained.
- 11:25 "devise devices": retained.
- 11:31 "forces shall stand on his part" (*zero'im mimmenu ya'amodu*): retained.
- 11:39 "strongest fortresses": retained.
- 11:42 "escape": retained.
- 11:36 "the indignation": the word of 8:19; retained.
- R8 lineation: none; the chapter is prose.
- R9: one layer throughout; no nested quotation. The discourse spans three chapters; closed and reopened at the break (00087).

## Daniel 11

---
1  “And as for me, in the first year of Darius the Mede, I stood up to confirm and strengthen him.[br]
2  And now will I show you the truth. Behold, there shall stand up yet three kings in Persia; and the fourth shall be far richer than they all: and when he has grown strong through his riches, he shall stir up all against the realm of Greece.[br]
→ three kings in Persia :: Ezra 4:5–7
3  And a mighty king shall stand up, that shall rule with great dominion, and do according to his will.[br]
→ a mighty king shall stand up :: Daniel 8:5–8; Daniel 8:21
4  And when he shall stand up, his kingdom shall be broken, and shall be divided toward the four winds of heaven, but not to his posterity, nor according to his dominion with which he ruled; for his kingdom shall be plucked up, even for others besides these.
→ divided toward the four winds of heaven :: Daniel 8:8; Daniel 7:2; Jeremiah 49:36; Zechariah 2:6
>>>
5  And the king of the south shall be strong, and one of his princes; and he shall be strong above him, and have dominion; his dominion shall be a great dominion.[br]
6  And at the end of years they shall join themselves together; and the daughter of the king of the south shall come to the king of the north to make an agreement: but she shall not retain the strength of her arm; neither shall he stand, nor his arm; but she shall be given up, and those who brought her, and the one who fathered her, and the one who strengthened her in those times.[br]
7  But out of a shoot from her roots shall one stand up in his place, who shall come to the army, and shall enter into the fortress of the king of the north, and shall deal against them, and shall prevail.[br]
→ a shoot from her roots :: Isaiah 11:1; Isaiah 60:21
» a shoot from her roots :: *netser mi-shorashehah* — the wording of Isaiah 11:1 (*netser mi-shorashav*), here of a royal branch.
8  And also their gods, with their molten images, and with their finest vessels of silver and of gold, shall he carry captive into Egypt; and he shall refrain some years from the king of the north.[br]
→ carry captive into Egypt :: Isaiah 46:1–2; Jeremiah 48:7; Jeremiah 49:3
9  And he shall come into the realm of the king of the south, but he shall return into his own land.[br]
10  And his sons shall war, and shall assemble a multitude of great forces, which shall come on, and overflow, and pass through; and they shall return and war, even to his fortress.[br]
→ overflow, and pass through :: Isaiah 8:8; Jeremiah 47:2; Daniel 11:26; Daniel 11:40
11  And the king of the south shall be moved with anger, and shall come forth and fight with him, even with the king of the north; and he shall set forth a great multitude, and the multitude shall be given into his hand.[br]
12  And the multitude shall be lifted up, and his heart shall be exalted; and he shall cast down tens of thousands, but he shall not prevail.[br]
→ his heart shall be exalted :: Deuteronomy 8:14; 2 Chronicles 26:16
13  And the king of the north shall return, and shall set forth a multitude greater than the former; and he shall come on at the end of the times, even of years, with a great army and with many possessions.[br]
→ with many possessions :: Genesis 15:14; Genesis 14:11
14  And in those times there shall many stand up against the king of the south: also the children of the violent among your people shall lift themselves up to establish the vision; but they shall fall.[br]
→ the children of the violent among your people :: Jeremiah 7:11; Ezekiel 18:10
15  So the king of the north shall come, and cast up a mound, and take a well-fortified city: and the forces of the south shall not stand, neither his chosen people, neither shall there be any strength to stand.[br]
→ cast up a mound :: 2 Kings 19:32; Jeremiah 6:6; Ezekiel 4:2
16  But he that comes against him shall do according to his own will, and none shall stand before him; and he shall stand in the glorious land, and in his hand shall be destruction.[br]
→ the glorious land :: Daniel 8:9; Ezekiel 20:6
17  And he shall set his face to come with the strength of his whole kingdom, and with him equitable conditions; and he shall perform them: and he shall give him the daughter of women, to ruin her; but she shall not stand, neither be for him.[br]
18  After this shall he turn his face to the isles, and shall take many: but a prince shall cause the reproach offered by him to cease; indeed, moreover, he shall cause his reproach to turn upon him.[br]
→ the isles :: Genesis 10:5; Isaiah 41:1; Jeremiah 31:10
» the isles :: *iyyim* — "isles, coastlands," the Mediterranean shores (Genesis 10:5; Isaiah 11:11; 66:19).
19  Then he shall turn his face toward the fortresses of his own land; but he shall stumble and fall, and shall not be found.[br]
→ shall not be found :: Psalm 37:36; Ezekiel 26:21
20  Then shall stand up in his place one that shall cause a tax collector to pass through the glory of the kingdom; but within a few days he shall be destroyed, neither in anger, nor in battle.
→ cause a tax collector to pass through the glory of the kingdom :: Exodus 5:6; 2 Kings 23:35; Zechariah 9:8
» a tax collector :: *noges* — "taskmaster, exactor" (Exodus 5:6, 5:13; Zechariah 9:8).
>>>
21  And in his place shall stand up a contemptible person, to whom they had not given the honor of the kingdom: but he shall come in time of security, and shall obtain the kingdom by flatteries.[br]
→ by flatteries :: Psalm 12:2–3; Proverbs 29:5
22  And the overwhelming forces shall be overwhelmed from before him, and shall be broken; indeed, also the prince of the covenant.[br]
→ the overwhelming forces shall be overwhelmed :: Isaiah 8:7–8; Nahum 1:8; Daniel 9:26
→ the prince of the covenant :: Daniel 9:25–27; Malachi 3:1; Isaiah 55:3–4
23  And after the alliance made with him he shall act deceitfully; for he shall come up, and shall become strong, with a small people.[br]
→ he shall act deceitfully :: Psalm 55:20–21
24  In time of security shall he come even upon the richest parts of the province; and he shall do that which his fathers have not done, nor his fathers’ fathers; he shall scatter among them prey, and spoil, and possessions: indeed, he shall devise his devices against the strongholds, even for a time.[br]
25  And he shall stir up his power and his courage against the king of the south with a great army; and the king of the south shall war in battle with an exceedingly great and mighty army; but he shall not stand; for they shall devise devices against him.[br]
26  Indeed, those who eat of his delicacies shall destroy him, and his army shall overflow; and many shall fall down slain.[br]
→ those who eat of his delicacies shall destroy him :: Psalm 41:9; Obadiah 1:7; Daniel 1:5
27  And as for both these kings, their hearts shall be to do harm, and they shall speak lies at one table: but it shall not prosper; for yet the end shall be at the time appointed.[br]
→ they shall speak lies at one table :: Psalm 28:3; Jeremiah 9:8
→ the end shall be at the time appointed :: Habakkuk 2:3; Daniel 8:19
28  Then shall he return into his land with great possessions; and his heart shall be against the holy covenant; and he shall do as he pleases, and return to his own land.[br]
→ the holy covenant :: Exodus 24:7–8; Leviticus 26:25; Daniel 9:27
29  At the time appointed he shall return, and come into the south; but it shall not be in the latter time as it was in the former.[br]
30  For ships of Kittim shall come against him; therefore he shall be grieved, and shall return, and have indignation against the holy covenant, and shall do as he pleases: he shall even return, and have regard to those who forsake the holy covenant.[br]
→ ships of Kittim :: Numbers 24:24; Genesis 10:4; Isaiah 23:1
» Kittim :: *Kittim* — a son of Javan (Genesis 10:4); in prophecy the western sea-peoples, in the same words as Numbers 24:24 (*tsim mi-yad Kittim*, "ships from the hand of Kittim").
31  And forces shall stand on his part, and they shall profane the sanctuary, even the fortress, and shall take away the continual burnt-offering, and they shall set up the abomination that makes desolate.[br]
→ they shall profane the sanctuary :: Ezekiel 24:21; Psalm 74:7
→ shall take away the continual burnt-offering :: Daniel 8:11; Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11
→ the abomination that makes desolate :: Daniel 8:13; Daniel 9:27; Daniel 12:11; Matthew 24:15
32  And such as do wickedly against the covenant shall he corrupt by flatteries; but the people who know their God shall be strong, and take action.[br]
→ the people who know their God :: Jeremiah 9:24; Hosea 6:3; Psalm 9:10
33  And those who are wise among the people shall instruct many; yet they shall fall by the sword and by flame, by captivity and by spoil, many days.[br]
→ those who are wise among the people shall instruct many :: Isaiah 52:13; Isaiah 53:11; Daniel 12:3
» those who are wise :: *maskilim* (from *sakhal*, "to act with insight") — in 11:33, 11:35; 12:3, 12:10; cf. Isaiah 52:13 (*yaskil*, "shall deal wisely").
34  Now when they shall fall, they shall be helped with a little help; but many shall join themselves to them with flatteries.[br]
35  And some of those who are wise shall fall, to refine them, and to purify, and to make them white, even to the time of the end; because it is yet for the time appointed.
→ to refine them, and to purify, and to make them white :: Malachi 3:2–3; Zechariah 13:9; Daniel 12:10; Isaiah 1:18
>>>
36  And the king shall do according to his will; and he shall exalt himself, and magnify himself above every god, and shall speak astonishing things against the God of gods; and he shall prosper till the indignation be accomplished; for that which is determined shall be done.[br]
→ he shall exalt himself, and magnify himself above every god :: Isaiah 14:13–14; Ezekiel 28:2; 2 Thessalonians 2:4
→ against the God of gods :: Deuteronomy 10:17; Daniel 2:47
» the God of gods :: *El elim* — "God of gods," the superlative of supremacy (Deuteronomy 10:17: *Elohei ha-elohim*; Daniel 2:47 in Aramaic).
→ till the indignation be accomplished :: Isaiah 10:25; Isaiah 26:20; Daniel 8:19
→ for that which is determined shall be done :: Isaiah 10:23; Isaiah 28:22; Daniel 9:26
37  Neither shall he regard the gods of his fathers, nor the desire of women, nor regard any god; for he shall magnify himself above all.[br]
→ the desire of women :: Ezekiel 8:14
38  But in his place shall he honor the god of fortresses; and a god whom his fathers knew not shall he honor with gold, and silver, and with precious stones, and pleasant things.[br]
→ a god whom his fathers knew not :: Deuteronomy 32:17; Jeremiah 44:3
» pleasant things :: *chamudot* — "desirable things," the root of 9:23, 10:3, 10:11.
39  And he shall deal with the strongest fortresses by the help of a foreign god: whoever acknowledges him he will increase with glory; and he shall cause them to rule over many, and shall divide the land for a price.[br]
40  And at the time of the end shall the king of the south contend with him; and the king of the north shall come against him like a whirlwind, with chariots,[br] and with horsemen,[br] and with many ships; and he shall enter into the countries, and shall overflow and pass through.[br]
→ like a whirlwind, with chariots :: Isaiah 5:28; Jeremiah 4:13
41  He shall enter also into the glorious land, and many countries shall be overthrown; but these shall be delivered out of his hand: Edom,[br] and Moab,[br] and the chief of the children of Ammon.[br]
→ but these shall be delivered out of his hand :: Isaiah 11:14; Jeremiah 9:25–26
42  He shall stretch forth his hand also upon the countries; and the land of Egypt shall not escape.[br]
43  But he shall have power over the treasures of gold and of silver, and over all the precious things of Egypt; and the Libyans and the Ethiopians shall be at his steps.[br]
→ the Libyans and the Ethiopians :: Nahum 3:9; Ezekiel 30:4–5; 2 Chronicles 16:8
» the Ethiopians :: *Kushim* ("Cushites") — Cush, the land south of Egypt (Genesis 10:6; Isaiah 18:1); *Lubim* ("Libyans"): Nahum 3:9.
44  But tidings out of the east and out of the north shall trouble him; and he shall go forth with great fury to destroy and utterly to sweep away many.[br]
→ tidings out of the east and out of the north shall trouble him :: 2 Kings 19:7; Jeremiah 51:46; Ezekiel 7:26
» utterly to sweep away :: *lehacharim* — "to put under the ban," from *cherem* (Deuteronomy 7:2; Joshua 6:17).
45  And he shall plant the tents of his palace between the sea and the glorious holy mountain; yet he shall come to his end, and none shall help him.”
→ the glorious holy mountain :: Daniel 9:16; Daniel 9:20; Psalm 48:1–2; Joel 2:1
» the tents of his palace :: *oholei apadno* — *apadno* ("his palace") is a Persian loanword, found only here in the Tanakh.
