## D. Reviewed — no change proposed

- **Policy — archaic word order, not proposed:** "make not known" (2:5, 2:9), "Destroy not" (2:24), "can neither wise men… show" (2:27), "Then was the iron…" (2:35), "shall the God of heaven set up" (2:44). Same as the Matthew caches.
- 2:9 "till the time be changed": subjunctive "be" retained (same policy).
- 2:10, 2:11 "that can show" (singular relative): only plural "them/they that" are converted under 00151.
- 2:2 "sorcerers" (*mekhashfin*), "enchanters" (*ashshafin*), "magicians" (*chartummin*), "soothsayers" (*gazrin*, "determiners of fate"): the diviner lists are retained as in ch.1.
- 2:5 "dunghill" (*nivli*): retained; the same word at Ezra 6:11.
- 2:14 "counsel and prudence": retained.
- 2:21 "times and seasons" (*iddanayya vizimnayya*): retained.
- 2:25 "children of the captivity": retained (idiomatic, Ezra 6:16).
- 2:41 "miry clay" (*chasaf tin*): retained.
- 2:43 "the seed of men" and "cleave": retained.
- 2:48 "chief governor" (*rav signin*): retained.
- R8 lineation: vv.20–23 are poetic (a doxology); the ASV's printed cola are not preserved in the working file, so no indented lines are proposed. If you want them, supply the cola breaks. Not touched.
- R9: speech balance checked per above; v.16, v.18 are reported speech (not quoted).
