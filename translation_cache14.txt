- "made whole" (14:36): kept per 9:21–22 (precedent applied at ch.9; *diesōthēsan*, "completely healed").
- "worshipped" (14:33): *prosekynēsan* — kept, as at 2:11, 8:2, 9:18, 15:25; ch.28 note at 28:9 discusses *proskynesis*. Corpus-wide treatment not reopened.
- 14:1 "At that season": kept, as at 11:25, 12:1. "tetrarch": a title, retained.
- 14:2 "servants" (*paisin*): kept.
- 14:3 "laid hold on" (*kratēsas*, "seized"): intelligible; kept.
- 14:8 "platter" (*pinaki*): living. "Give me here": intelligible; kept.
- 14:9 "the king": Herod Antipas was tetrarch, Matthew's "king" is loose usage — kept as in Greek (*ho basileus*).
- 14:9 oaths: Lev. 5:4 and Judges 11:35 (rash vows) considered; not proposed (weak against "one strong insight per verse").
- 14:19 "sit down" (*anaklithēnai*, "recline"): kept — 00013 targets table-posture "sit at meat"; on grass, "sit down" is adequate. Flag if you'd prefer "recline."

## E. Full proposed chapter text (Matthew 14)

## Matthew 14

---
1  At that season Herod the tetrarch heard the report concerning Yeshua,
2  and said to his servants, “This is John the Immerser; he has been raised from the dead; and therefore these powers are at work in him.”[br]
3  For Herod had laid hold on John, and bound him, and put him in prison for the sake of Herodias, his brother Philip’s wife.[br]
→ for the sake of Herodias :: 1 Kings 19:1–2
4  For John said to him, “It is not lawful for you to have her.”[br]
» It is not lawful for you to have her :: Marrying a living brother's wife is forbidden under Leviticus 18:16 and 20:21; the levirate duty of Deuteronomy 25:5 applies only after a brother has died childless, which was not Herodias's case.
5  And when he wanted to put him to death, he feared the multitude, because they counted him as a prophet.
6  But when Herod’s birthday came, the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst, and pleased Herod.[br]
» Herod’s birthday :: Birthday celebrations were a Greco-Roman custom, not a native Jewish one (cf. Genesis 40:20's Pharaoh, the only other biblical birthday feast) — the detail marks the Herodian court's assimilation to pagan practice.
7  So he promised with an oath to give her whatever she asked.[br]
8  And she, being prompted by her mother, says, “Give me here on a platter the head of John the Immerser.”[br]
9  And the king was grieved; but for the sake of his oaths, and of those who reclined at the table with him, he commanded it to be given;[br]
10  and he sent and beheaded John in the prison.[br]
11  And his head was brought on a platter, and given to the girl: and she brought it to her mother.
12  And his disciples came, and took up the corpse, and buried him; and they went and told Yeshua.
---
13  Now when Yeshua heard it, He withdrew from there in a boat, to a desolate place apart: and when the multitudes heard of it, they followed Him on foot from the cities.
14  And He came forth, and saw a great multitude, and He had compassion on them, and healed their sick.
→ healed their sick :: Ezekiel 34:4, 16
15  And when evening had come, the disciples came to Him, saying, “The place is desolate, and the hour has already passed; send the multitudes away, that they may go into the villages, and buy themselves food.”[br]
→ The place is desolate :: Psalm 78:19–20
16  But Yeshua said to them, “They have no need to go away; you all give them something to eat.”[br]
→ give them something to eat :: 2 Kings 4:42–44
17  And they say to Him, “We have here but five loaves, and two fish.”[br]
18  And He said, “Bring them here to Me.”[br]
19  And He commanded the multitudes to sit down on the grass; and He took the five loaves, and the two fish, and looking up to heaven, He blessed, and broke and gave the loaves to the disciples, and the disciples to the multitudes.[br]
→ sit down on the grass :: Psalm 23:2
» looking up to heaven, He blessed :: The customary table blessing (*berakhah*) precedes the meal, not the food itself — the same form and posture recurring at the Last Supper (26:26).
20  And they all ate, and were filled: and they took up what was left over of the broken pieces, twelve baskets full.[br]
21  And those who ate were about five thousand men, besides women and children.
→ about five thousand men, besides women and children :: Exodus 12:37; Numbers 11:21–22
---
22  And immediately He made the disciples get into the boat and go ahead of Him to the other side, until He had sent the multitudes away.
23  And after He had sent the multitudes away, He went up into the mountain apart to pray: and when evening had come, He was there alone.
24  But the boat was now in the midst of the sea, distressed by the waves; for the wind was contrary.[br]
25  And in the fourth watch of the night He came to them, walking upon the sea.[br]
→ walking upon the sea :: Job 9:8; Psalm 77:19
26  And when the disciples saw Him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, “It is a ghost”; and they cried out for fear.[br]
27  But immediately Yeshua spoke to them, saying, “Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid.”[br]
→ be not afraid :: Isaiah 41:10; 43:1–2
» it is I :: *egō eimi* behind the Greek — the same self-identifying formula echoing YHWH's self-naming to Moses (Exodus 3:14, LXX); on the sea, where mastery belongs to YHWH alone (Job 9:8, Psalm 107:29), Yeshua applies the divine formula to Himself.
28  And Peter answered Him and said, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You upon the waters.”[br]
29  And He said, “Come.” And Peter went down from the boat, and walked upon the waters to come to Yeshua.
30  But when he saw the wind, he was afraid; and as he began to sink, he cried out, saying, “Lord, save me.”[br]
→ Lord, save me :: Psalm 69:1–2
31  And immediately Yeshua stretched forth His hand, and took hold of him, and says to him, “O you of little faith, why did you doubt?”[br]
→ stretched forth His hand :: Psalm 144:7; Psalm 18:16
32  And when they had gone up into the boat, the wind ceased.
→ the wind ceased :: Psalm 107:29; Jonah 1:15
33  And those who were in the boat worshipped Him, saying, “Truly You are the Son of God.”
---
34  And when they had crossed over, they came to the land, to Gennesaret.
35  And when the men of that place knew Him, they sent into all that region round about, and brought to Him all that were sick;[br]
36  and they begged Him that they might only touch the fringe of His garment: and as many as touched were made whole.
→ the fringe of His garment :: Numbers 15:38–39; Malachi 4:2
