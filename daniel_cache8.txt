## D. Reviewed — no change proposed

- **Policy — archaic word order, not proposed:** "touched not the ground" (8:5), "shut up" is the only fix at 8:26.
- 8:4 "magnified himself" (*higdil*, "grew great"), 8:8, 8:11, 8:25: retained.
- 8:7 "moved with anger" (*mitmarmar*): retained.
- 8:9 "the glorious land": retained (see the » note; "beautiful" would follow 00055, but *ha-tsevi* is a title, not an adjective).
- 8:13 "a holy one…another holy one" (*qadosh*): retained; no "saint".
- 8:13 "to be trodden under foot": retained.
- 8:20, 8:21 "Media and Persia", "Greece": place names kept (§6), with the *Yavan* note.
- 8:22 "stand up out of the nation": retained.
- 8:24 "not by his own power": retained.
- 8:27 "did the king's business": retained.
- R8 lineation: none in ch.8.
- R9: Gabriel's speech (19–26) is one layer; no nested quotation.

New `---` before v.1, v.15 (Gabriel appears), v.27 (aftermath). `>>>` paragraph breaks before v.3 (the vision proper) and v.20 (the interpretation). `[br]` on direction lists (vv.4, 9) and at speech line-ends. No `[br]` before a break marker.

## Flags outside chapter 8 (no action taken)

- "did his pleasure" recurs 11:3, 11:16, 11:36; "dark sentences" none elsewhere in Daniel after 5:12; "wonderfully" none.
- "prince of the host" / "prince of princes" are the only divine "prince" titles; 10:13, 10:20–21, 12:1 name angelic "princes" (lowercase, not proposed).
- "holy people" (8:24, 12:7) kept; the 7:18 "saints" decision is separate.

1  In the third year of the reign of king Belshazzar a vision appeared to me, even to me, Daniel, after that which appeared to me at the first.
→ In the third year of the reign of king Belshazzar :: Daniel 7:1
2  And I saw in the vision; now it was so, that when I saw, I was in Shushan the palace, which is in the province of Elam; and I saw in the vision, and I was by the river Ulai.
→ Shushan the palace :: Nehemiah 1:1; Esther 1:2
→ the province of Elam :: Isaiah 21:2; Jeremiah 49:34–39
» the river Ulai :: *'ubal Ulai* — *'ubal* is a watercourse or canal; the "banks of the Ulai" in v.16.
>>>
3  Then I lifted up my eyes, and saw, and, behold, there stood before the river a ram which had two horns: and the two horns were high; but one was higher than the other, and the higher came up last.
4  I saw the ram pushing westward,[br] and northward,[br] and southward; and no beasts could stand before him, neither was there any that could deliver out of his hand; but he did according to his will, and magnified himself.
5  And as I was considering, behold, a he-goat came from the west over the face of the whole earth, and touched not the ground: and the goat had a conspicuous horn between his eyes.
6  And he came to the ram that had the two horns, which I saw standing before the river, and ran upon him in the fury of his power.
7  And I saw him come close to the ram, and he was moved with anger against him, and struck the ram, and broke his two horns; and there was no power in the ram to stand before him; but he cast him down to the ground, and trampled upon him; and there was none that could deliver the ram out of his hand.
8  And the he-goat magnified himself exceedingly: and when he was strong, the great horn was broken; and instead of it there came up four conspicuous horns toward the four winds of heaven.
→ toward the four winds of heaven :: Daniel 11:4; Zechariah 2:6
9  And out of one of them came forth a little horn, which grew exceedingly great, toward the south,[br] and toward the east,[br] and toward the glorious land.
→ the glorious land :: Jeremiah 3:19; Ezekiel 20:6; Ezekiel 20:15; Daniel 11:16; Daniel 11:41; Daniel 11:45
» the glorious land :: *ha-tsevi* ("the beauty, the glory"; also "gazelle") — a title of the land in Ezekiel 20:6, 20:15 and Jeremiah 3:19.
10  And it grew great, even to the host of heaven; and some of the host and of the stars it cast down to the ground, and trampled upon them.
→ some of the host and of the stars it cast down to the ground :: Isaiah 14:12–13; Genesis 15:5; Genesis 22:17; Daniel 12:3
11  Indeed, it magnified itself, even to the Prince of the host; and it took away from Him the continual burnt-offering, and the place of His sanctuary was cast down.
→ the Prince of the host :: Joshua 5:14–15
» the Prince of the host :: *sar ha-tseva* — "commander of the host"; cf. *sar tseva YHWH* ("commander of YHWH's host," Joshua 5:14–15).
→ the continual burnt-offering :: Exodus 29:38–42; Numbers 28:3–8; Daniel 9:27; Daniel 11:31; Daniel 12:11
12  And the host was given over to it together with the continual burnt-offering through transgression; and it cast down truth to the ground, and it did as it pleased and prospered.
→ it cast down truth to the ground :: Isaiah 59:14–15
13  Then I heard a holy one speaking; and another holy one said to that certain one who spoke, “How long shall be the vision concerning the continual burnt-offering, and the transgression that makes desolate, to give both the sanctuary and the host to be trodden under foot?”
→ How long shall be the vision :: Psalm 74:9–10; Isaiah 6:11; Daniel 12:6
→ the transgression that makes desolate :: Daniel 9:27; Daniel 11:31; Daniel 12:11
→ trodden under foot :: Isaiah 63:18; Psalm 79:1
» that certain one :: *palmoni* — "a certain one" (cf. *peloni almoni*, "so-and-so," Ruth 4:1); some read "the numberer." A hapax in the Tanakh.
14  And he said to me, “To two thousand and three hundred evenings and mornings; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed.”
→ evenings and mornings :: Genesis 1:5
» evenings and mornings :: *'erev boqer* — "evening-morning," the formula for a day in Genesis 1:5, 1:8, 1:13.
» shall the sanctuary be cleansed :: *nitsdaq* (root *ts-d-q*, "be set right, vindicated"; the root of "righteous").
→ shall the sanctuary be cleansed :: 2 Chronicles 29:15–18; Daniel 9:24

15  And it happened, when I, even I Daniel, had seen the vision, that I sought to understand it; and, behold, there stood before me as the appearance of a man.
→ the appearance of a man :: Ezekiel 1:26; Daniel 10:5; Daniel 10:16; Daniel 10:18
16  And I heard a man’s voice between the banks of the Ulai, which called, and said, “Gabriel, make this man to understand the vision.”
→ Gabriel :: Daniel 9:21; Luke 1:19; Luke 1:26
» Gabriel :: *Gavri'el* — "God is my strong man" (*gever* + *El*); the first angelic name in the Tanakh (also 9:21).
→ between the banks of the Ulai :: Daniel 12:6–7; Daniel 10:5
17  So he came near where I stood; and when he came, I was terrified, and fell upon my face: but he said to me, “Understand, O son of man; for the vision belongs to the time of the end.”
→ O son of man :: Ezekiel 2:1; Ezekiel 3:1
→ the vision belongs to the time of the end :: Habakkuk 2:3; Daniel 11:35; Daniel 12:4; Daniel 12:9
18  Now as he was speaking with me, I fell into a deep sleep with my face toward the ground; but he touched me, and set me upright.
→ I fell into a deep sleep :: Genesis 2:21; Genesis 15:12
→ he touched me, and set me upright :: Ezekiel 2:2; Daniel 10:10

19  And he said, “Behold, I will make you know what shall be in the latter time of the indignation; for it belongs to the appointed time of the end.[br]
→ the latter time of the indignation :: Isaiah 26:20; Isaiah 10:25
20  The ram which you saw, that had the two horns, they are the kings of Media and Persia.[br]

» Greece :: *Yavan* ("Javan") — the son of Japheth in Genesis 10:2, 10:4; the Hebrew name for Greece (Isaiah 66:19; Ezekiel 27:13; Zechariah 9:13).

23  And in the latter time of their kingdom, when the transgressors have reached their full measure, a king of fierce countenance, and understanding riddles, shall stand up.[br]
