# Mark

## Mark 1

1  The beginning of the good news of Yeshua Messiah, the Son of God.[br]
2  Even as it is written in Isaiah the prophet, “Behold, I send my messenger before Your face,
      Who shall prepare Your way;
→ I send my messenger before Your face :: Malachi 3:1
» Even as it is written in Isaiah the prophet :: Mark fuses Malachi 3:1 and Isaiah 40:3 under a single citation formula naming only Isaiah — the more prominent prophet lending his name to the combined quotation, a recognized ancient citation convention, not an error.
3  The voice of one crying in the wilderness,
      Make you all ready the way of the Lord,
      Make His paths straight;”[br]
→ The voice of one crying in the wilderness :: Isaiah 40:3
4  John came, who immersed in the wilderness and preached the immersion of repentance to remission of sins.
» the immersion of repentance :: *teshuvah* ("repentance, turning") — John's immersion draws on the existing Second Temple practice of ritual washing for purification (*mikveh*), but redirects it: not repeated ceremonial cleansing, but a one-time turning in view of the coming kingdom.
5  And there went out to him all the country of Judea, and all they of Jerusalem; and they were immersed of him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins.
6  And John was clothed with camel’s hair, and had a leather belt around his waist, and did eat locusts and wild honey.[br]
» a leather belt around his waist :: Similar to the description of Elijah in 2 Kings 1:8.
7  And he preached, saying, “There comes after me He that is mightier than I, the strap of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and unloose.
8  I immersed you in water; but He shall immerse you in the Holy Spirit.”
---
9  And it happened in those days, that Yeshua came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was immersed of John in the Jordan.
10  And immediately coming up out of the water, He saw the heavens rent asunder, and the Spirit as a dove descending upon Him:
11  and a voice came out of the heavens, “You are my beloved Son, in You I am well pleased.”[br]
» You are my beloved Son, in You I am well pleased :: Combines two Hebrew scriptures — the anointing of Psalm 2:7 ("today I have begotten you") and the Servant's commissioning in Isaiah 42:1 ("my chosen, in whom my soul delights"); the Father names Yeshua Messiah and suffering Servant in a single sentence.
→ You are my beloved Son :: Psalm 2:7
→ in You I am well pleased :: Isaiah 42:1
---
12  And immediately the Spirit drives Him forth into the wilderness.
13  And He was in the wilderness forty days tempted of Satan; and He was with the wild beasts; and the angels ministered to Him.
---
14  Now after John was delivered up, Yeshua came into Galilee, preaching the good news of God,
15  and saying, “The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand: repent you all, and believe in the good news.”
---
16  And passing along by the sea of Galilee, He saw Simon and Andrew the brother of Simon casting a net in the sea; for they were fishers.
17  And Yeshua said to them, “Come you all after me, and I will make you to become fishers of men.”[br]
18  And immediately they left the nets, and followed Him.
19  And going on a little further, He saw Jacob the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, who also were in the boat mending the nets.
20  And immediately He called them: and they left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired servants, and went after Him.
---
21  And they go into Capernaum; and immediately on the sabbath day He entered into the synagogue and taught.
» And they go into Capernaum :: *erchontai* ("they go/come") — a historical present, Mark's habit of narrating past events in the present tense for vividness, occurring roughly 151 times in his Gospel against a handful in Luke, who regularly converts Mark's presents to the aorist to suit his more literary, ordered historiographical register (cf. Luke 1:1–4). Watch for the switching between present- and past-tense verbs throughout the narrative from here on — it is a feature of Mark's Greek, not an inconsistency in translation.
22  And they were astonished at His teaching: for He taught them as having authority, and not as the scribes.
23  And immediately there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; and he cried out,
24  saying, “What have we to do with You, Yeshua You Nazarene? Have You come to destroy us? I know you who You are, the Holy One of God.”[br]
» I know you who You are, the Holy One of God :: Naming an exorcist's opponent by his true identity was itself a weapon in ancient adjuration practice; Yeshua's counter-command silences the spirit rather than answering it, reversing the expected contest.
25  And Yeshua rebuked him, saying, “Hold your peace, and come out of him.”[br]
26  And the unclean spirit, tearing him and crying with a loud voice, came out of him.
27  And they were all amazed, so that they questioned among themselves, saying, “What is this? a new teaching! with authority He commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey Him.”[br]
28  And the report of him went out immediately everywhere into all the region of Galilee round about.
---
29  And immediately, when they were come out of the synagogue, they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with Jacob and John.
30  Now Simon’s wife’s mother lay sick of a fever; and immediately they tell Him of her:
31  and He came and took her by the hand, and raised her up; and the fever left her, and she ministered to them.[br]
32  And at even, when the sun did set, they brought to Him all that were sick, and them that were possessed with demons.
33  And all the city was gathered together at the door.
34  And He healed many that were sick with various diseases, and cast out many demons; and He suffered not the demons to speak, because they knew him.
---
35  And in the morning, a great while before day, He rose up and went out, and departed into a desolate place, and there prayed.
36  And Simon and they that were with Him followed after Him;
37  and they found Him, and say to Him, “All are seeking you.”[br]
38  And He says to them, “Let us go elsewhere into the next towns, that I may preach there also; to this end came I forth.”[br]
39  And He went into their synagogues throughout all Galilee, preaching and casting out demons.
---
40  And there comes to Him a leper, begging Him, and kneeling down to Him, and saying to Him, “If You will, You can make me clean.”[br]
41  And being moved with compassion, He stretched forth His hand, and touched him, and says to him, “I will; be you made clean.”[br]
» being moved with compassion :: *splagchnistheis* ("moved in the inward parts," i.e. with compassion) is the majority reading; a minority of witnesses (Codex Bezae, some Old Latin) read *orgistheis* ("moved with anger") instead — some text critics judge the harder, less expected reading more likely original, with "compassion" a scribal softening.
42  And immediately the leprosy departed from him, and he was made clean.
43  And He strictly charged him, and immediately sent him out,

→ the things which Moses commanded :: Leviticus 14:2–4
45  But he went out, and began to proclaim it much, and to spread abroad the matter, so that Yeshua could no more openly enter into a city, but was without in desolate places: and they came to him from every quarter.

## Mark 2

2  And many were gathered together, so that there was no longer room for them, no, not even about the door: and He spoke the word to them.
3  And they come, bringing to Him a paralyzed man, borne of four.
4  And when they could not come near to Him for the crowd, they uncovered the roof where He was: and when they had broken it up, they let down the bed on which the paralyzed man lay.[br]
5  And Yeshua seeing their faith says to the paralyzed man, “Son, your sins are forgiven.”[br]
6  But there were certain of the scribes sitting there, and reasoning in their hearts,
7  “Why does this man thus speak? He blasphemes: who can forgive sins but one, even God?”[br]
8  And immediately Yeshua, perceiving in His spirit that they so reasoned within themselves, says to them, “Why reason you all these things in your hearts?
9  Which is easier, to say to the paralyzed man, ‘Your sins are forgiven’; or to say, ‘Arise, and take up your bed, and walk’?
10  But that you all may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins (He says to the paralyzed man),

11  I say to you, Arise, take up your bed, and go to your house.”[br]
12  And he arose, and immediately took up the bed, and went forth before them all; so that they were all amazed, and glorified God, saying, “We never saw it on this fashion.”

13  And He went forth again by the sea side; and all the multitude came to Him, and He taught them.

» began, as they went, to pick the ears :: Picking grain by hand was permitted by written Torah (Deuteronomy 23:25); the charge rests on the oral law's thirty-nine categories of forbidden sabbath work (m. Shabbat 7:2), under which picking counted as reaping and rubbing as threshing.
24  And the Pharisees said to Him, “Behold, why do they on the sabbath day that which is not lawful?”[br]

→ save for the priests :: Leviticus 24:9
27  And He said to them, “The sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath:
» The sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath :: Here Yeshua asserts His superior authority above the oral tradition, an unconscionable idea for the Pharisees and their man made religious system.
28  so that the Son of man is lord even of the sabbath.”

## Mark 3

1  And He entered again into the synagogue; and there was a man there who had his hand withered.
2  And they watched Him, whether he would heal him on the sabbath day; that they might accuse Him.
» that they might accuse Him :: An escalation in the narrative.  Chapters 1 and 2 the religious leaders listen and question Yeshua, now, here in chapter 3, they are openly looking for an opportunity to be hostile with Him.  The overwhelming sadness is that Yeshua is serving the needs of others, and the religious leaders are only concerned with upholding their traditions in the face of great need. 
3  And He says to the man that had his hand withered, “Stand forth.”[br]
4  And He says to them, “Is it lawful on the sabbath day to do good, or to do harm? to save a life, or to kill?” But they held their peace.[br]
5  And when He had looked round about on them with anger, being grieved at the hardening of their heart, He says to the man, “Stretch forth your hand.” And he stretched it forth; and his hand was restored.[br]
6  And the Pharisees went out, and immediately with the Herodians took counsel against Him, how they might destroy Him.
» with the Herodians took counsel against Him :: Pharisees and Herodians were natural opponents — Torah-zealous separatists against a client dynasty's supporters — an alliance formed only against a shared threat, a measure of how quickly Yeshua's sabbath rulings had escalated.

9  And He spoke to his disciples, that a little boat should wait on Him because of the crowd, lest they should press in on him:
10  for He had healed many; so that as many as had plagues pressed upon Him that they might touch Him.

15  and to have authority to cast out demons:

## Mark 4

1  And again He began to teach by the sea side. And there is gathered to Him a very great multitude, so that He entered into a boat, and sat in the sea; and all the multitude were by the sea on the land.
2  And He taught them many things in parables, and said to them in His teaching,
3  “Listen: Behold, the sower went forth to sow:[br]
4  and it happened, as he sowed, some seed fell by the way side, and the birds came and devoured it.
5  And other fell on the rocky ground, where it had not much earth; and immediately it sprang up, because it had no deepness of earth:
6  and when the sun was risen, it was scorched; and because it had no root, it withered away.[br]
7  And other fell among the thorns, and the thorns grew up, and choked it, and it yielded no fruit.[br]
8  And others fell into the good ground, and yielded fruit, growing up and increasing; and brought forth, thirtyfold, and sixtyfold, and a hundredfold.[br]
9  And He said, Who has ears to hear, let him hear.”[br]
10  And when He was alone, they that were about Him with the twelve asked of Him the parables.
11  And He said to them, “To you is given the mystery of the kingdom of God: but to them that are without, all things are done in parables:
12  that seeing they may see, and not perceive;
      and hearing they may hear, and not understand;
      lest perhaps they should turn again, and it should be forgiven them.”[br]
→ that seeing they may see, and not perceive :: Isaiah 6:9–10
13  And He says to them, “Know you all not this parable? and how shall you all know all the parables?
14  The sower sows the word.
15  And these are they by the way side, where the word is sown; and when they have heard, immediately comes Satan, and takes away the word which has been sown in them.[br]
16  And these in like manner are they that are sown upon the rocky places, who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with joy;
17  and they have no root in themselves, but endure for a while; then, when tribulation or persecution arises because of the word, immediately they stumble.[br]
18  And others are they that are sown among the thorns; these are they that have heard the word,
19  and the cares of the world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in, choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.[br]
20  And those are they that were sown upon the good ground; such as hear the word, and accept it, and bear fruit, thirtyfold, and sixtyfold, and a hundredfold.”[br]
21  And He said to them, “Is the lamp brought to be put under the bushel, or under the bed, and not to be put on the stand?
22  For there is nothing hid, save that it should be manifested; neither was anything made secret, but that it should come to light.[br]
23  If any man has ears to hear, let him hear.”[br]
24  And he said to them, “Take heed what you all hear: with what measure you all measure it shall be measured to you; and more shall be given to you.
25  For he that has, to him shall be given: and he that has not, from him shall be taken away even that which he has.”[br]
26  And He said, “So is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast seed upon the earth;
27  and should sleep and rise night and day, and the seed should spring up and grow, he knows not how.
28  The earth bears fruit of herself; first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear.
29  But when the fruit is ripe, immediately he puts forth the sickle, because the harvest is come.”[br]
30  And He said, “How shall we liken the kingdom of God? or in what parable shall we set it forth?
31  It is like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown upon the earth, though it be less than all the seeds that are upon the earth,
32  yet when it is sown, grows up, and becomes greater than all the herbs, and puts out great branches; so that the birds of the heaven can lodge under the shadow thereof.”[br]
33  And with many such parables spoke He the word to them, as they were able to hear it;
34  and without a parable spoke He not to them: but privately to His own disciples He expounded all things.
---
35  And on that day, when even was come, He says to them, “Let us go over to the other side.”[br]
36  And leaving the multitude, they take Him with them, even as he was, in the boat. And other boats were with Him.
37  And there arises a great storm of wind, and the waves beat into the boat, so that the boat was now filling.
38  And He Himself was in the stern, asleep on the cushion: and they awake Him, and say to Him, “Teacher, care you not that we perish?”
39  And He awoke, and rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, “Peace, be still.” And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.[br]
» Peace, be still :: The Greek command *pephimōso* ("be muzzled") is the same verb Yeshua used to silence the unclean spirit at 1:25 — the sea is addressed as an unruly power to be bound, not merely calmed.  There seems to be a sense that the storm was caused by demonic activity.
40  And He said to them, “Why are you all fearful? have you all not yet faith?”[br]
41  And they feared exceedingly, and said one to another, “Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?”[br]
» that even the wind and the sea obey him :: In the Hebrew scriptures mastery over the sea belongs to YHWH alone (Job 38:11, Psalm 107:29).

## Mark 5

## Mark 6

1  And He went out from there; and He comes into His own country; and His disciples follow Him.
2  And when the sabbath was come, He began to teach in the synagogue: and many hearing Him were astonished, saying, “From where has this man these things? and, What is the wisdom that is given to this man, and what are such works of power coming about through His hands?
