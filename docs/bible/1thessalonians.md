# 1 Thessalonians

## 1 Thessalonians 1

1  Paul, and Silvanus, and Timothy, to the assembly of the Thessalonians in God the Father and the Lord Yeshua Messiah: Grace to you and peace.
2  We give thanks to God always for you all, making mention of you in our prayers;
3  remembering without ceasing your work of faith and labor of love and patience of hope in our Lord Yeshua Messiah, before our God and Father;
4  knowing, brothers beloved of God, your election,
5  how that our good news came not to you in word only, but also in power, and in the Holy Spirit, and in much assurance; even as you all know what manner of men we showed ourselves toward you for your sake.
---
6  And you all became imitators of us, and of the Lord, having received the word in much affliction, with joy of the Holy Spirit;
7  so that you all became an example to all that believe in Macedonia and in Achaia.
8  For from you has sounded forth the word of the Lord, not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but in every place your faith to God-ward is gone forth; so that we need not to speak anything.
9  For they themselves report concerning us what manner of entering in we had to you; and how you all turned to God from idols, to serve a living and true God,
→ a living and true God :: Jeremiah 10:10
» — the phrase echoes the Tanakh's polemic against idols, where YHWH alone is called "the true God" (*elohim emet*) and "the living God" (*elohim chayyim*, Jeremiah 10:10; cf. Psalm 115:3–7); the Gentile convert's turn "from idols" to serve this God is the missionary counterpart to Israel's Shema-confession.
10  and to wait for His Son from heaven, whom He raised from the dead, even Yeshua, who delivers us from the wrath to come.

## 1 Thessalonians 2

1  For yourselves, brothers, know our entering in to you, that it has not been found vain:
2  but having suffered before and been shamefully treated, as you all know, at Philippi, we grew bold in our God to speak to you the good news of God in much conflict.
3  For our exhortation is not of error, nor of uncleanness, nor in guile:
4  but even as we have been approved of God to be intrusted with the good news, so we speak; not as pleasing men, but God who proves our hearts.
5  For neither at any time were we found using words of flattery, as you all know, nor a cloak of covetousness, God is witness;
6  nor seeking glory of men, neither from you nor from others, when we might have claimed authority as apostles of Messiah.
7  But we were gentle in the midst of you, as when a nurse cherishes her own children:
8  even so, being affectionately desirous of you, we were well pleased to impart to you, not the good news of God only, but also our own souls, because you all were become very dear to us.
9  For you all remember, brothers, our labor and travail: working night and day, that we might not burden any of you, we preached to you the good news of God.
10  You all are witnesses, and God also, how holily and righteously and unblamably we behaved ourselves toward you that believe:
11  as you all know how we dealt with each one of you, as a father with his own children, exhorting you, and encouraging you, and testifying,
12  to the end that you all should walk worthily of God, who calls you into His own kingdom and glory.
---
13  And for this cause we also thank God without ceasing, that, when you all received from us the word of the message, even the word of God, you all accepted it not as the word of men, but, as it is in truth, the word of God, which also works in you that believe.
14  For you all, brothers, became imitators of the assemblies of God which are in Judæa in Messiah Yeshua: for you all also suffered the same things of your own countrymen, even as they did of the Jews;
15  who both killed the Lord Yeshua and the prophets, and drove out us, and please not God, and are contrary to all men;
16  forbidding us to speak to the Gentiles that they may be saved; to fill up their sins always: but the wrath is come upon them to the uttermost.
---
17  But we, brothers, being bereaved of you for a short season, in presence not in heart, endeavored the more exceedingly to see your face with great desire:
18  because we would gladly have come to you, I Paul once and again; and Satan hindered us.
19  For what is our hope, or joy, or crown of glorying? Are not even you all, before our Lord Yeshua at His coming?
» — "crown of glorying" draws on the Tanakh's reward-crown imagery (*ateret tiferet*, "a crown of glory," Proverbs 16:31; Isaiah 28:5, of the righteous remnant); Paul names the Thessalonians themselves as his crown before the Lord at His coming.
20  For you all are our glory and our joy.

## 1 Thessalonians 3

1  Therefore when we could no longer forbear, we thought it good to be left behind at Athens alone;
2  and sent Timothy, our brother and God's minister in the good news of Messiah, to establish you, and to comfort you concerning your faith;
3  that no man be moved by these afflictions; for yourselves know that hereunto we are appointed.
4  For truly, when we were with you, we told you beforehand that we are to suffer affliction; even as it happened, and you all know.
5  For this cause I also, when I could no longer forbear, sent that I might know your faith, lest by any means the tempter had tempted you, and our labor should be in vain.
---
6  But when Timothy came even now to us from you, and brought us glad tidings of your faith and love, and that you all have good remembrance of us always, longing to see us, even as we also to see you;
7  for this cause, brothers, we were comforted over you in all our distress and affliction through your faith:
8  for now we live, if you all stand fast in the Lord.
9  For what thanksgiving can we render again to God for you, for all the joy with which we joy for your sakes before our God;
10  night and day praying exceedingly that we may see your face, and may perfect that which is lacking in your faith?
---
11  Now may our God and Father Himself, and our Lord Yeshua, direct our way to you:
12  and the Lord make you to increase and abound in love one toward another, and toward all men, even as we also do toward you;
13  to the end He may establish your hearts unblamable in holiness before our God and Father, at the coming of our Lord Yeshua with all His saints.
→ with all His saints :: Zechariah 14:5
» — "with all his saints" echoes Zechariah 14:5's theophany, "and YHWH my God shall come, and all the holy ones with him"; Paul applies the same coming-with-holy-ones expectation to the coming of the Lord Yeshua.

## 1 Thessalonians 4

1  Finally then, brothers, we beseech and exhort you in the Lord Yeshua, that, as you all received of us how you all ought to walk and to please God, even as you all do walk,—that you all abound more and more.
2  For you all know what charge we gave you through the Lord Yeshua.
3  For this is the will of God, even your sanctification, that you all abstain from fornication;
4  that each one of you know how to possess himself of his own vessel in sanctification and honor,
5  not in the passion of lust, even as the Gentiles who know not God;
6  that no man transgress, and wrong his brother in the matter: because the Lord is an avenger in all these things, as also we forewarned you and testified.
7  For God called us not for uncleanness, but in sanctification.
8  Therefore he that rejects, rejects not man, but God, who gives His Holy Spirit to you.
---
9  But concerning love of the brothers you all have no need that one write to you: for you all yourselves are taught of God to love one another;
10  for indeed you all do it toward all the brothers that are in all Macedonia. But we exhort you, brothers, that you all abound more and more;
11  and that you all study to be quiet, and to do your own business, and to work with your hands, even as we charged you;
12  that you all may walk becomingly toward them that are without, and may have need of nothing.
---
13  But we would not have you ignorant, brothers, concerning them that fall asleep; that you all sorrow not, even as the rest, who have no hope.
14  For if we believe that Yeshua died and rose again, even so them also that are fallen asleep in Yeshua will God bring with Him.
15  For this we say to you by the word of the Lord, that we that are alive, that are left to the coming of the Lord, shall by no means precede them that are fallen asleep.
16  For the Lord Himself shall descend from heaven, with a shout,[br] with the voice of the archangel,[br] and with the trump of God: and the dead in Messiah shall rise first;
→ with the trump of God :: Isaiah 27:13
» — "the trump of God" recalls two registers at once: the shofar-blast of the Sinai theophany (Exodus 19:16, 19) and the "great trumpet" that gathers Israel's scattered in the last days (Isaiah 27:13); Paul applies the same ingathering-trumpet imagery to the resurrection and gathering of believers to the Lord.
17  then we that are alive, that are left, shall together with them be caught up in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air: and so shall we ever be with the Lord.
18  Therefore comfort one another with these words.

## 1 Thessalonians 5

1  But concerning the times and the seasons, brothers, you all have no need that anything be written to you.
2  For yourselves know perfectly that the day of the Lord so comes as a thief in the night.
→ the day of the Lord :: Zephaniah 1:14
» *yom YHWH* ("the day of the LORD") — the fixed prophetic term for the coming day of judgment and reckoning (Joel 2:1; Amos 5:18; Zephaniah 1:14–15); Paul assumes the term needs no explanation to his readers and simply supplies its unpredictable, thief-like arrival.
3  When they are saying, Peace and safety, then sudden destruction comes upon them, as travail upon a woman with child; and they shall by no means escape.
---
4  But you all, brothers, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief:
5  for you all are all sons of light, and sons of the day: we are not of the night, nor of darkness;
6  so then let us not sleep, as do the rest, but let us watch and be sober.
7  For they that sleep sleep in the night; and they that are drunken are drunken in the night.
8  But let us, since we are of the day, be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love; and for a helmet, the hope of salvation.
→ the breastplate of faith and love; and for a helmet, the hope of salvation :: Isaiah 59:17
» — the same pairing as Isaiah 59:17, where YHWH the warrior arms himself with righteousness for a breastplate and salvation for a helmet — already read messianically at Ephesians 6:14–17 — here becomes the believer's own equipment as a child "of the day."
9  For God appointed us not to wrath, but to the obtaining of salvation through our Lord Yeshua Messiah,
10  who died for us, that, whether we wake or sleep, we should live together with Him.
11  Therefore exhort one another, and build each other up, even as also you all do.
---
12  But we beseech you, brothers, to know them that labor among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you;
13  and to esteem them exceeding highly in love for their work's sake. Be at peace among yourselves.
14  And we exhort you, brothers, admonish the disorderly, encourage the fainthearted, support the weak, be longsuffering toward all.
15  See that none render to any one evil for evil; but always follow after that which is good, one toward another, and toward all.
---
16  Rejoice always;[br]
17  pray without ceasing;[br]
18  in everything give thanks: for this is the will of God in Messiah Yeshua to you-ward.[br]
19  Quench not the Spirit;[br]
20  despise not prophesyings;[br]
21  prove all things; hold fast that which is good;[br]
22  abstain from every form of evil.
---
23  And the God of peace Himself sanctify you wholly; and may your spirit and soul and body be preserved entire, without blame at the coming of our Lord Yeshua Messiah.
24  Faithful is He that calls you, who will also do it.
---
25  Brothers, pray for us.
26  Salute all the brothers with a holy kiss.
27  I adjure you by the Lord that this epistle be read to all the brothers.
28  The grace of our Lord Yeshua Messiah be with you.
