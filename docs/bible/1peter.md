# 1 Peter

## 1 Peter 1

1  Peter, an apostle of Yeshua Messiah, to the elect who are sojourners of the Dispersion in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,
» Peter :: *Petros* (Πέτρος), "Rock" — the Greek rendering of the Aramaic *Kepha* (כֵּיפָא), the name Yeshua gave Simon bar-Jonah (John 1:42; Matthew 16:18). His given name was Simon; "Peter" is the name Yeshua conferred, not a birth name.
» sojourners of the Dispersion :: *parepidēmois diasporas* (παρεπιδήμοις διασπορᾶς) — "Dispersion" is technical vocabulary for Jews scattered outside the land of Israel (cf. John 7:35). Jacob addresses "the twelve tribes... of the Dispersion" the same way (James 1:1); Peter opens to the same Jewish Diaspora audience, scattered through the named provinces of Asia Minor.
2  according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, in sanctification of the Spirit, to obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Yeshua Messiah: Grace to you and peace be multiplied.
→ sprinkling of the blood :: Exodus 24:8
» sprinkling of the blood :: recalls the Sinai covenant-ratification, where Moses sprinkled sacrificial blood on the people and they answered, "All that YHWH has spoken we will do" (Exodus 24:7-8). Peter opens with a triadic pattern — the Father's foreknowledge, the Spirit's sanctifying, Messiah's blood — casting his Diaspora readers as a covenant people.
>>>
3  Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Yeshua Messiah, who according to His great mercy begat us again to a living hope by the resurrection of Yeshua Messiah from the dead,
» Blessed be :: *eulogētos* (εὐλογητός) opens a berakah, the Jewish blessing-formula (Hebrew *barukh*) that opens many Psalms and the synagogue Amidah. Peter frames his opening praise in the cadence of Jewish liturgy.
4  to an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fades not away, reserved in heaven for you,
5  who by the power of God are guarded through faith to a salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.
>>>
6  In which you all greatly rejoice, though now for a little while, if need be, you all have been put to grief in manifold trials,
→ manifold trials :: James 1:2-3
» manifold trials :: *poikilois peirasmois* (ποικίλοις πειρασμοῖς) — the same phrase, word order reversed, as Jacob's "trials of many kinds" (James 1:2). Two independent letters to Jewish believers reach for the identical construction to describe the testing of faith.
7  that the proof of your faith, being more precious than gold that perishes though it is proved by fire, may be found to praise and glory and honor at the revelation of Yeshua Messiah:
8  whom not having seen you all love; on whom, though now you all see Him not, yet believing, you all rejoice greatly with joy unspeakable and full of glory:
9  receiving the culmination of your faith, even the salvation of your souls.
» culmination of your faith :: *telos* (τέλος) — the same root as *tetelestai* (τετέλεσται), "it is finished," Yeshua's word from the cross (John 19:30). A commercial term for a debt "paid in full" — reaching the intended goal, not merely stopping. Faith's *telos* here is its completion, not its expiration.
>>>
10  Concerning which salvation the prophets sought and searched diligently, who prophesied of the grace that should come to you:
11  searching what time or what manner of time the Spirit of Messiah which was in them did point to, when it testified beforehand the sufferings of Messiah, and the glories that should follow them.
» the Spirit of Messiah which was in them :: locates Messiah's own Spirit active within the OT prophets, moving them to search their own oracles of a suffering-then-glorified Messiah (Isaiah 53's stricken servant set against Isaiah 9's enthroned king) without yet seeing how the two converge in one Person.
12  To whom it was revealed, that not to themselves, but to you, did they minister these things, which now have been announced to you through them that preached the good news to you by the Holy Spirit sent forth from heaven; which things angels desire to look into.
>>>
13  Therefore girding up the loins of your mind, [br] be sober [br] and set your hope perfectly on the grace that is to be brought to you at the revelation of Yeshua Messiah;
» girding up the loins :: a Hebraic idiom — gathering the long outer robe up into the belt to work or move unhindered (cf. Exodus 12:11, eaten "with your loins girded" at the first Passover). Peter calls for the same readiness, applied to the mind.
14  as children of obedience, [br] not fashioning yourselves according to your former lusts in the time of your ignorance:
15  but like as He who called you is holy, [br] be you all yourselves also holy in all manner of living;
16  because it is written, “You all shall be holy; for I am holy.”
→ You all shall be holy; for I am holy :: Leviticus 19:2
17  And if you all call on Him as Father, who without respect of persons judges according to each one’s work, [br] pass the time of your sojourning in fear:
18  knowing that you all were redeemed, not with corruptible things, with silver or gold, [br] from your vain manner of life handed down from your fathers;
» vain manner of life handed down from your fathers :: read within Peter's Jewish frame, this need not mean pagan idolatry — it more naturally targets inherited human tradition layered over Torah (the "tradition of the elders," cf. Mark 7:1-13), the same target of Yeshua's own controversies with the Pharisees.
19  but with precious blood, as of a lamb without spot, [br] even the blood of Messiah:
→ a lamb without spot :: Exodus 12:5
» a lamb without spot :: fuses the unblemished Passover lamb (Exodus 12:5) with the suffering servant "led as a lamb to the slaughter" (Isaiah 53:7) — two lamb-images collapsed into one.
20  who was foreknown indeed before the foundation of the world, [br] but was manifested at the end of the times for your sake,
21  who through Him are believers in God, that raised Him from the dead, and gave Him glory; [br] so that your faith and hope might be in God.
>>>
22  Seeing you all have purified your souls in your obedience to the truth to unfeigned love of the brothers, [br] love one another from the heart fervently:
23  having been begotten again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, [br] through the word of God, which lives and abides.
24  For, [br] “All flesh is as grass, [br] And all the glory thereof as the flower of grass. [br] The grass withers, and the flower falls: [br]
→ All flesh is as grass, And all the glory thereof as the flower of grass :: Isaiah 40:6-7
25  But the word of the Lord abides for ever.” [br] And this is the word of good tidings which was preached to you.
→ the word of the Lord abides for ever :: Isaiah 40:8
» word of the Lord :: quotes Isaiah 40:6-8, where the Hebrew reads "the word of our God" (*devar-Eloheinu*); the Greek text Peter cites has *kyriou*, "of the Lord." The shift from "God" to "Lord" sits naturally beside Peter's habit elsewhere in this chapter of applying divine language to Yeshua.

## 1 Peter 2

1  Putting away therefore all wickedness, [br] and all guile, [br] and hypocrisies, [br] and envies, [br] and all evil speakings,
2  as newborn babes, long for the spiritual milk which is without guile, that you all may grow thereby to salvation;
» spiritual milk :: *logikon* (λογικόν) — the same root as *logos* ("word"), echoing "the word of God, which lives and abides" (1:23) and "the word of good tidings" (1:25). The milk that makes a newborn believer grow is explicitly Word-milk.
3  if you all have tasted that the Lord is gracious:
→ if you all have tasted that the Lord is gracious :: Psalm 34:8
>>>
4  to whom coming, a living stone, rejected indeed of men, but with God elect, precious,
» a living stone, rejected indeed of men, but with God elect, precious :: opens a three-verse catena (vv6-8) of stone-texts — Isaiah 28:16, Psalm 118:22, Isaiah 8:14 — the rejected-but-chosen pattern early Messianic preaching returns to again and again (cf. Acts 4:11).
5  you all also, as living stones, are built up a spiritual house, to be a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to God through Yeshua Messiah.
6  Because it is contained in scripture, [br] “Behold, I lay in Zion a chief corner stone, elect, precious: And he that believes on Him shall not be put to shame.”
→ Behold, I lay in Zion a chief corner stone, elect, precious :: Isaiah 28:16
7  For you therefore that believe is the preciousness: but for such as disbelieve, [br] “The stone which the builders rejected, The same was made the head of the corner”;
→ The stone which the builders rejected, The same was made the head of the corner :: Psalm 118:22
8  and, [br] “A stone of stumbling, and a rock of offence”; [br] for they stumble at the word, being disobedient: to which also they were appointed.
→ A stone of stumbling, and a rock of offence :: Isaiah 8:14
>>>
9  But you all are an elect race, [br] a royal priesthood, [br] a holy nation, [br] a people for God’s own possession, that you all may show forth the excellencies of Him who called you out of darkness into His marvellous light:
→ an elect race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation :: Exodus 19:6
» an elect race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for God’s own possession :: quotes Israel’s own Sinai covenant title (Exodus 19:6, “a kingdom of priests, and a holy nation”) and Isaiah 43:20-21’s “people whom I formed for myself.” This is the single strongest textual signal of Peter’s audience: he does not say his readers have received a new title grafted in from outside — he addresses them as bearers of Israel’s own covenant identity.
10  who in time past were no people, but now are the people of God: who had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.
→ who in time past were no people, but now are the people of God :: Hosea 2:23
» were no people, but now are the people of God :: quotes Hosea’s Lo-Ammi/Lo-Ruhamah oracle (Hosea 1:6-10; 2:23) — Israel, put away for unfaithfulness, restored and renamed “my people,” “her that had obtained mercy.” Peter applies Israel’s own promised restoration to his readers’ return to covenant standing.
>>>
11  Beloved, I beseech you as sojourners and pilgrims, [br] to abstain from fleshly lusts, which war against the soul;
→ sojourners and pilgrims :: Psalm 39:12
12  having your behavior seemly among the Gentiles; that, in which they speak against you as evil-doers, they may by your good works, which they behold, glorify God in the day of visitation.
» among the Gentiles :: *ethnesin* (ἔθνεσιν) — Peter sets his readers apart from “the Gentiles,” the same distinction Torah and the prophets draw between Israel and the nations; naming a group “Gentiles” reads oddly from an author writing to Gentiles about other Gentiles.
>>>
13  Be subject to every ordinance of man for the Lord’s sake: whether to the king, as supreme;
14  or to governors, as sent by him for vengeance on evil-doers and for praise to them that do well.
15  For so is the will of God, that by well-doing you all should put to silence the ignorance of foolish men:
16  as free, and not using your freedom for a cloak of wickedness, but as bondservants of God.
17  Honor all men. [br] Love the brotherhood. [br] Fear God. [br] Honor the king.
>>>
18  Servants, be in subjection to your masters with all fear; not only to the good and gentle, but also to the harsh.
» harsh :: *skolios* (σκολιός), "crooked/perverse" — contrasted with *epieikēs* ("gentle/reasonable") in the same verse.
19  For this is acceptable, if for conscience toward God a man endures griefs, suffering wrongfully.
20  For what glory is it, if, when you all sin, and are buffeted for it, you all shall take it patiently? but if, when you all do well, and suffer for it, you all shall take it patiently, this is acceptable with God.
>>>
21  For hereunto were you all called: because Messiah also suffered for you, leaving you an example, that you all should follow His steps:
22  who did no sin, neither was guile found in His mouth:
→ neither was guile found in His mouth :: Isaiah 53:9
23  who, when He was reviled, reviled not again; when He suffered, threatened not; but committed Himself to Him that judges righteously:
24  who His own self bore our sins in His body upon the tree, [br] that we, having died to sins, might live to righteousness; [br] by whose stripes you all were healed.
→ by whose stripes you all were healed :: Isaiah 53:5
25  For you all were going astray like sheep; [br] but are now returned to the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls.
→ you all were going astray like sheep :: Isaiah 53:6

## 1 Peter 3

1  In like manner, you all wives, [br] be in subjection to your own husbands; that, even if any obey not the word, they may without the word be gained by the behavior of their wives;
2  beholding your chaste behavior coupled with fear.
>>>
3  Whose adorning let it not be the outward adorning of braiding the hair, and of wearing jewels of gold, or of putting on apparel;
4  but let it be the hidden man of the heart, in the incorruptible apparel of a meek and quiet spirit, which is in the sight of God of great price.
5  For after this manner aforetime the holy women also, who hoped in God, adorned themselves, being in subjection to their own husbands:
6  as Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord: whose children you all now are, if you all do well, and are not put in fear by any terror.
→ Sarah obeyed Abraham, calling him lord :: Genesis 18:12
>>>
7  You all husbands, in like manner, dwell with your wives according to knowledge, giving honor to the woman, as to the weaker vessel, as being also joint-heirs of the grace of life; to the end that your prayers be not hindered.
» the weaker vessel :: *asthenesterō skeuei* — physically weaker, not morally or spiritually; immediately balanced by “joint-heirs of the grace of life” — equal standing before God alongside a difference in strength.
>>>
8  Finally, be you all all likeminded, [br] compassionate, [br] loving as brothers, [br] tenderhearted, [br] humbleminded:
9  not rendering evil for evil, or reviling for reviling; [br] but contrariwise blessing; [br] for hereunto were you all called, that you all should inherit a blessing.
>>>
10  For, [br] “He that would love life, And see good days, Let him refrain his tongue from evil, And his lips that they speak no guile: [br]
11  And let him turn away from evil, and do good; Let him seek peace, and pursue it. [br]
12  For the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, And His ears to their supplication: But the face of the Lord is upon them that do evil.”
→ the eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous :: Psalm 34:12-16
» Peter’s second citation of this psalm in the letter (cf. 2:3, “you all have tasted that the Lord is gracious,” Psalm 34:8) — a psalm of David’s own deliverance from danger, applied here to a persecuted Diaspora readership.
>>>
13  And who is he that will harm you, if you all be zealous of that which is good?
14  But even if you all should suffer for righteousness’ sake, blessed are you all: [br] and fear not their fear, [br] neither be troubled;
→ fear not their fear, neither be troubled :: Isaiah 8:12
15  but sanctify in your hearts Messiah as Lord: [br] being ready always to give answer to every man that asks you a reason concerning the hope that is in you, [br] yet with meekness and fear:
→ sanctify in your hearts Messiah as Lord :: Isaiah 8:13
» sanctify in your hearts Messiah as Lord :: Isaiah 8:13 reads “sanctify YHWH of hosts Himself” — Peter directs Isaiah’s charge to sanctify YHWH toward Messiah, naming Him “Lord” in the same breath.
16  having a good conscience; that, in which you all are spoken against, they may be put to shame who revile your good manner of life in Messiah.
17  For it is better, if the will of God should so will, that you all suffer for well-doing than for evil-doing.
>>>
18  Because Messiah also suffered for sins once, [br] the righteous for the unrighteous, [br] that He might bring us to God; [br] being put to death in the flesh, but made alive in the spirit;
19  in which also He went and preached to the spirits in prison,
» spirits in prison :: the same phrase-world as 2 Peter 2:4 (“angels that sinned... committed to pits of darkness”) and Jude 6 (“angels that kept not their own principality... in everlasting bonds”), both drawing on the Second Temple Enoch tradition (1 Enoch 6-16) of the fallen Watchers imprisoned before the flood. What Peter means by “went and preached” to them is debated; the imagery He is drawing on is not.
20  that aforetime were disobedient, when the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was a preparing, in which few, that is, eight souls, were saved through water:
21  which also after a true likeness does now save you, even immersion, not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the interrogation of a good conscience toward God, through the resurrection of Yeshua Messiah;
» a true likeness :: *antitypon* (ἀντίτυπον), “antitype” — a typological term (its only other NT use is Hebrews 9:24): the flood water that carried Noah’s ark is the *type*; immersion is its *antitype*, the pattern’s fulfillment.
22  who is on the right hand of God, having gone into heaven; angels and authorities and powers being made subject to Him.
→ on the right hand of God :: Psalm 110:1

## 1 Peter 4

1  Since, then, Messiah suffered in the flesh, arm you all yourselves also with the same mind; for he that has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin;
2  that you all no longer should live the rest of your time in the flesh to the lusts of men, but to the will of God.
>>>
3  For the time past may suffice to have wrought the desire of the Gentiles, [br] and to have walked in lasciviousness, [br] lusts, [br] winebibbings, [br] wild parties, [br] carousings, [br] and abominable idolatries:
» wild parties :: *kōmois* (κώμοις) — distinct from "carousings" (*potois*, drinking-bouts) beside it; *kōmos* is the noisy, public revel/orgy.
4  in which they think it strange that you all run not with them into the same excess of riot, speaking evil of you:
5  who shall give account to Him that is ready to judge the living and the dead.
6  For to this end was the good news preached even to the dead, that they might be judged indeed according to men in the flesh, but live according to God in the spirit.
» preached even to the dead :: the same crux as 3:19’s “spirits in prison” — whether this names a specific proclamation to Noah’s generation, or states more generally that the good news reaches every generation, including those already dead when Peter writes.
>>>
7  But the end of all things is at hand: be you all therefore of sound mind, and be sober to prayer:
8  above all things being fervent in your love among yourselves; for love covers a multitude of sins:
→ love covers a multitude of sins :: Proverbs 10:12
9  using hospitality one to another without murmuring:
10  according as each has received a gift, ministering it among yourselves, [br] as good stewards of the manifold grace of God;
11  if any man speaks, speaking as it were oracles of God; [br] if any man ministers, ministering as of the strength which God supplies: [br] that in all things God may be glorified through Yeshua Messiah, whose is the glory and the dominion for ever and ever. Amen.
>>>
12  Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trial among you, which comes upon you to prove you, as though a strange thing happened to you:
» the fiery trial :: *pyrōsei* — the same refining-fire image opened at 1:7 (“gold that perishes though it is proved by fire”), now closing back over the letter.
13  but insomuch as you all are partakers of Messiah’s sufferings, rejoice; that at the revelation of His glory also you all may rejoice with exceeding joy.
14  If you all are reproached for the name of Messiah, blessed are you all; because the Spirit of glory and the Spirit of God rests upon you.
→ the Spirit of glory and the Spirit of God rests upon you :: Isaiah 11:2
» rests upon you :: *anapauetai* — the same verb as Isaiah 11:2, “the Spirit of YHWH shall rest [*anapausetai*] upon Him,” now said of Peter’s readers.
>>>
15  For let none of you suffer as a murderer, [br] or a thief, [br] or an evil-doer, [br] or as a meddler in other men’s matters:
16  but if a man suffer as a Christian, let him not be ashamed; but let him glorify God in this name.
» as a Christian :: *Christianos* — the name given by outsiders (see Acts 11:26), “the Messiah-people.” Peter’s own use of it here, without comment, shows the label had already stuck to Yeshua’s followers by the time he writes.
>>>
17  For the time is come for judgment to begin at the house of God: and if it begin first at us, what shall be the end of them that obey not the good news of God?
18  And if the righteous is scarcely saved, where shall the ungodly and sinner appear?
→ if the righteous is scarcely saved, where shall the ungodly and sinner appear :: Proverbs 11:31
19  Therefore let them also that suffer according to the will of God commit their souls in well-doing to a faithful Creator.

## 1 Peter 5

1  The elders therefore among you I exhort, who am a fellow-elder, and a witness of the sufferings of Messiah, who am also a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed:
2  Tend the flock of God which is among you, [br] exercising the oversight, not of constraint, but willingly, according to the will of God; [br] nor yet for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind;
3  neither as lording it over the charge allotted to you, but making yourselves examples to the flock.
>>>
4  And when the chief Shepherd shall be manifested, you all shall receive the crown of glory that fades not away.
» the chief Shepherd :: bookends 2:25’s “the Shepherd and Overseer of your souls” — the elders (v2, “tend the flock”) act as under-shepherds to the one Peter already named the Shepherd.
>>>
5  Likewise, you all younger, be subject to the elder. [br] Indeed, all of you gird yourselves with humility, to serve one another: [br] for God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble.
→ God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble :: Proverbs 3:34
» God resists the proud, but gives grace to the humble :: the same citation of Proverbs 3:34 that closes Jacob’s letter to the same effect (James 4:6) — two independent letters reaching for the identical verse to press the same point.
6  Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, [br] that He may exalt you in due time;
→ the mighty hand of God :: Deuteronomy 5:15
7  casting all your anxiety upon Him, because He cares for you.
→ casting all your anxiety upon Him, because He cares for you :: Psalm 55:22
>>>
8  Be sober, be watchful: your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walks about, seeking whom he may devour,
» adversary :: *satan* (שָׂטָן) — Hebrew for “adversary/accuser,” the title of the prosecuting figure in Job 1-2 and Zechariah 3:1-2.
9  whom withstand steadfast in your faith, knowing that the same sufferings are accomplished in your brothers who are in the world.
>>>
10  And the God of all grace, who called you to His eternal glory in Messiah, after that you all have suffered a little while, shall Himself perfect, [br] establish, [br] strengthen you.
11  To Him be the dominion for ever and ever. Amen.
>>>
12  By Silvanus, our faithful brother, as I account him, I have written to you briefly, exhorting, and testifying that this is the true grace of God: stand you all fast therein.
» Silvanus :: the Latinized name of Silas, Paul’s companion in Acts 15-18 (cf. 2 Corinthians 1:19, 1 Thessalonians 1:1) — evidently now working alongside Peter as well, and likely his amanuensis for this letter (dictation-secretaries were standard practice for NT letters).
13  She that is in Babylon, elect together with you, salutes you; and so does Mark my son.
» Babylon :: read literally by some (a genuine Jewish Diaspora community lived in Babylon into the medieval period) and as a cipher for Rome by others, following the same code Revelation 17-18 uses; early tradition (e.g. Eusebius) favors Rome. “Mark my son” is most naturally John Mark, the Gospel-writer (cf. Acts 12:12, 15:37-39; Colossians 4:10; 2 Timothy 4:11) — a son in discipleship rather than by birth.
14  Salute one another with a kiss of love. Peace be to you all that are in Messiah.
