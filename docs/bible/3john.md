# 3 John

## 3 John 1

1  The elder to Gaius the beloved, whom I love in truth.
2  Beloved, I pray that in all things you may prosper and be in health, even as your soul prospers.
3  For I rejoiced greatly, when brothers came and bore witness to your truth, even as you walk in truth.
4  Greater joy have I none than this, to hear of my children walking in the truth.
>>>
5  Beloved, you do a faithful work in whatever you do toward them that are brothers and strangers besides;
6  who bore witness to your love before the assembly: whom you will do well to set forward on their journey worthily of God:
7  because that for the sake of the Name they went forth, taking nothing of the Gentiles.
» for the sake of the Name :: *hyper tou onomatos* — "the Name" used absolutely, without further specification, follows the Jewish reverential habit of speaking of God's name (*ha-Shem*) without pronouncing it directly; here it is extended to Yeshua's name as the ground of these itinerant workers' mission.
8  We therefore ought to welcome such, that we may be fellow-workers for the truth.
---
9  I wrote somewhat to the assembly: but Diotrephes, who loves to have the preeminence among them, receives us not.
» loves to have the preeminence :: *philoprōteuōn* ("loving to be first") — an early, concrete instance of a single leader seizing authority over a local assembly against the apostolic circle, rather than an abstract warning.
10  Therefore, if I come, I will bring to remembrance his works which he does, prating against us with wicked words: and not content therewith, neither does he himself receive the brothers, and them that would he forbids and casts them out of the assembly.
11  Beloved, imitate not that which is evil, but that which is good. He that does good is of God: he that does evil has not seen God.
---
12  Demetrius has the witness of all men, and of the truth itself: indeed, we also bear witness; and you know that our witness is true.
» has the witness of all men, and of the truth itself :: three witnesses named for Demetrius — echoing, in commendation rather than accusation, the Torah's requirement that a matter stand "at the mouth of two or three witnesses" (cf. Deuteronomy 19:15, cited directly at 1 Timothy 5:19 for the opposite case, an accusation against an elder).
>>>
13  I had many things to write to you, but I am unwilling to write them to you with ink and pen:
14  but I hope shortly to see you, and we shall speak face to face. Peace be to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.
