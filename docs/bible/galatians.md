# Galatians

## Galatians 1

1  Paul, an apostle (not from men, neither through man, but through Yeshua Messiah, and God the Father, who raised Him from the dead),
2  and all the brothers that are with me, to the assemblies of Galatia:[br]
3  Grace to you and peace from God the Father, and our Lord Yeshua Messiah,
4  who gave Himself for our sins, that He might deliver us out of this present evil world, according to the will of our God and Father:
5  to whom be the glory for ever and ever.[br] Amen.
---
6  I marvel that you all are so quickly removing from Him that called you in the grace of Messiah to a different good news;
7  which is not another good news: only there are some that trouble you, and would pervert the good news of Messiah.
8  But though we, or an angel from heaven, should preach to you any good news other than that which we preached to you, let him be anathema.
» *anathema* ("accursed / devoted to destruction") — the Greek renders Hebrew *cherem*, the ban that consigns a thing irrevocably to God for destruction (Joshua 6:17–18); Paul places a false good news under the same absolute interdiction.
9  As we have said before, so say I now again, If any man preaches to you any good news other than that which you all received, let him be anathema.
10  For am I now seeking the favor of men, or of God? [br] Or am I striving to please men? If I were still pleasing men, I should not be a servant of Messiah.
---
11  For I make known to you, brothers, as touching the good news which was preached by me, that it is not after man.
12  For neither did I receive it from man, nor was I taught it, but it came to me through revelation of Yeshua Messiah.
13  For you all have heard of my manner of life in time past in the Jews’ religion, how that beyond measure I persecuted the assembly of God, and made havoc of it:
14  and I advanced in the Jews’ religion beyond many of my own age among my countrymen, being more exceedingly zealous for the traditions of my fathers.
» *paradoseis* ("traditions") — the *masorot ha-avot*, the Pharisaic oral tradition received and handed on (*masar*, m. Avot 1:1); Paul writes as a trained transmitter within that chain, not an outsider to it.
15  But when it was the good pleasure of God, who separated me, even from my mother’s womb, and called me through His grace,
→ separated me, even from my mother’s womb :: Jeremiah 1:5
» — the language of separation from the womb and calling by grace casts Paul's commission in the mold of the prophetic call formulas of Jeremiah 1:5 and Isaiah 49:1, the servant "called from the womb" and sent to the nations.
16  to reveal His Son in me, that I might preach Him among the Gentiles; immediately I conferred not with flesh and blood:
17  neither went I up to Jerusalem to them that were apostles before me: but I went away into Arabia; and again I returned to Damascus.
18  Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to visit Cephas, and tarried with him fifteen days.
19  But other of the apostles saw I none, save Jacob the Lord’s brother.
20  Now touching the things which I write to you, behold, before God, I lie not.
21  Then I came into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.
22  And I was still unknown by face to the assemblies of Judæa which were in Messiah:
23  but they only heard say, “He that once persecuted us now preaches the faith of which he once made havoc”;
24  and they glorified God in me.

## Galatians 2

1  Then after the space of fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus also with me.
2  And I went up by revelation; and I laid before them the good news which I preach among the Gentiles but privately before them who were of repute, lest by any means I should be running, or had run, in vain.
3  But not even Titus who was with me, being a Greek, was compelled to be circumcised:
4  and that because of the false brothers privily brought in, who came in privily to spy out our liberty which we have in Messiah Yeshua, that they might bring us into bondage:
5  to whom we gave place in the way of subjection, no, not for an hour; that the truth of the good news might continue with you.
6  But from those who were reputed to be somewhat (whatever they were, it makes no matter to me: God accepts not man’s person)—they, I say, who were of repute imparted nothing to me:
7  but contrariwise, when they saw that I had been intrusted with the good news of the uncircumcision, even as Peter with the good news of the circumcision
8  (for He that wrought for Peter to the apostleship of the circumcision wrought for me also to the Gentiles);
9  and when they perceived the grace that was given to me, Jacob and Cephas and John, they who were reputed to be pillars, gave to me and Barnabas the right hands of fellowship, that we should go to the Gentiles, and they to the circumcision;
10  only they would that we should remember the poor; which very thing I was also zealous to do.
---
11  But when Cephas came to Antioch, I resisted him to the face, because he stood condemned.
12  For before that certain came from Jacob, he ate with the Gentiles; but when they came, he drew back and separated himself, fearing them that were of the circumcision.
» — table fellowship was a halakhic flashpoint: eating with Gentiles risked contracting ritual impurity and sharing untithed or improperly prepared food, so the circumcision party withdrew; Peter's retreat conceded that Jew and Gentile could not sit at one table in Messiah.
13  And the rest of the Jews dissembled likewise with him; insomuch that even Barnabas was carried away with their dissimulation.
14  But when I saw that they walked not uprightly according to the truth of the good news, I said to Cephas before them all, “If you, being a Jew, live as do the Gentiles, and not as do the Jews, how compel you the Gentiles to live as do the Jews?”
---
15  We being Jews by nature, and not sinners of the Gentiles,
16  yet knowing that a man is not justified by the works of the Torah but through faith in Yeshua Messiah, even we believed on Messiah Yeshua, that we might be justified by faith in Messiah, and not by the works of the Torah: because by the works of the Torah shall no flesh be justified.
» *erga nomou* ("works of the Torah") — the same phrase, *ma'asei ha-torah*, titles the Qumran halakhic letter 4QMMT, where observing specified commandments marks covenant standing; Paul denies that such works, rather than faith, justify.
17  But if, while we sought to be justified in Messiah, we ourselves also were found sinners, is Messiah a minister of sin? God forbid.
18  For if I build up again those things which I destroyed, I prove myself a transgressor.
19  For I through the Torah died to the Torah, that I might live to God.
20  I have been crucified with Messiah; and it is no longer I that live, but Messiah lives in me: and that life which I now live in the flesh I live in faith, the faith which is in the Son of God, who loved me, and gave Himself up for me.
21  I do not make void the grace of God: for if righteousness is through the Torah, then Messiah died for nought.

## Galatians 3

1  O foolish Galatians, who did bewitch you, before whose eyes Yeshua Messiah was openly set forth crucified?
2  This only would I learn from you, Received you all the Spirit by the works of the Torah, or by the hearing of faith?
3  Are you all so foolish? having begun in the Spirit, are you all now perfected in the flesh?
4  Did you all suffer so many things in vain? if it be indeed in vain.
5  He therefore that supplies to you the Spirit, and works miracles among you, does He it by the works of the Torah, or by the hearing of faith?
---
6  Even as Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned to him for righteousness.
→ Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned to him for righteousness :: Genesis 15:6
7  Know therefore that they that are of faith, the same are sons of Abraham.
8  And the scripture, foreseeing that God would justify the Gentiles by faith, preached the good news beforehand to Abraham, saying, “In you shall all the nations be blessed.”
→ In you shall all the nations be blessed :: Genesis 12:3
9  So then they that are of faith are blessed with the faithful Abraham.
10  For as many as are of the works of the Torah are under a curse: for it is written, “Cursed is every one who continues not in all things that are written in the book of the Torah, to do them.”
→ Cursed is every one who continues not :: Deuteronomy 27:26
11  Now that no man is justified by the Torah before God, is evident: for, “The righteous shall live by faith”;
→ The righteous shall live by faith :: Habakkuk 2:4
» *emunah* ("faith / faithfulness") — b. Makkot 24a records Rabbi Simlai's teaching that Habakkuk reduced the whole Torah to one principle, "the righteous shall live by his faith"; Paul makes that single verse the hinge of justification.
12  and the Torah is not of faith; but, “He that does them shall live in them.”
→ He that does them shall live in them :: Leviticus 18:5
13  Messiah redeemed us from the curse of the Torah, having become a curse for us; for it is written, “Cursed is every one that hangs on a tree”:
→ Cursed is every one that hangs on a tree :: Deuteronomy 21:23
» — Deuteronomy 21:23 pronounced the hanged corpse *qilelat Elohim* ("a curse of God"); the Temple Scroll (11QTemple 64) already applied the verse to crucifixion, so Paul reads the cross as Messiah bearing the Torah's own curse.
14  that upon the Gentiles might come the blessing of Abraham in Messiah Yeshua; that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.
---
15  Brothers, I speak after the manner of men: Though it be but a man’s covenant, yet when it has been confirmed, no one makes it void, or adds thereto.
16  Now to Abraham were the promises spoken, and to his seed. He says not, “And to seeds,” as of many; but as of one, “And to your seed,” which is Messiah.
→ And to your seed :: Genesis 13:15
» *zera* ("seed") — Paul's argument turns on grammatical number, a recognized midrashic technique; the collective noun is read as a pointed singular to identify the one seed, the Messiah.
17  Now this I say: A covenant confirmed beforehand by God, the Torah, which came four hundred and thirty years after, does not disannul, so as to make the promise of none effect.
→ four hundred and thirty years :: Exodus 12:40
18  For if the inheritance is of the Torah, it is no more of promise: but God has granted it to Abraham by promise.
---
19  What then is the Torah? It was added because of transgressions, till the seed should come to whom the promise has been made; and it was ordained through angels by the hand of a mediator.
» — Second Temple tradition held the Torah was mediated by angels (Deuteronomy 33:2 LXX, Jubilees 1:27–29, Josephus Ant. 15.136); Paul invokes it to rank the Torah below the promise God spoke to Abraham directly.
20  Now a mediator is not a mediator of one; but God is one.
21  Is the Torah then against the promises of God? God forbid: for if there had been a Torah given which could make alive, truly righteousness would have been of the Torah.
22  But the scripture shut up all things under sin, that the promise by faith in Yeshua Messiah might be given to them that believe.
23  But before faith came, we were kept in ward under the Torah, shut up to the faith which should afterwards be revealed.
24  So that the Torah is become our tutor to bring us to Messiah, that we might be justified by faith.
25  But now that faith is come, we are no longer under a tutor.
26  For you all are all sons of God, through faith, in Messiah Yeshua.
27  For as many of you as were immersed into Messiah did put on Messiah.
28  There can be neither Jew nor Greek,[br] there can be neither bond nor free,[br] there can be no male and female; for you all are one man in Messiah Yeshua.
29  And if you all are Messiah’s, then are you all Abraham’s seed, heirs according to promise.

## Galatians 4

1  But I say that so long as the heir is a child, he differs nothing from a bondservant though he is lord of all;
2  but is under guardians and stewards until the day appointed of the father.
3  So we also, when we were children, were held in bondage under the rudiments of the world:
4  but when the fulness of the time came, God sent forth His Son, born of a woman, born under the Torah,
5  that He might redeem them that were under the Torah, that we might receive the adoption of sons.
6  And because you all are sons, God sent forth the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying, “Abba, Father.”
7  So that you are no longer a bondservant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir through God.
---
8  However at that time, not knowing God, you all were in bondage to them that by nature are no gods:
9  but now that you all have come to know God, or rather to be known by God, how turn you all back again to the weak and beggarly rudiments, to what you all desire to be in bondage over again?
10  You all observe days, and months, and seasons, and years.
» — the days, months, seasons, and years are the Jewish sacred calendar — Sabbaths, new moons (*rosh chodesh*), the appointed festivals (*mo'adim*), and the sabbatical years; Paul warns that adopting them as grounds of standing returns the Gentile believer to bondage.
11  I am afraid of you, lest by any means I have bestowed labor upon you in vain.
---
12  I beseech you, brothers, become as I am, for I also am become as you all are. You all did me no wrong:
13  but you all know that because of an infirmity of the flesh I preached the good news to you the first time:
14  and that which was a temptation to you in my flesh you all despised not, nor rejected; but you all received me as an angel of God, even as Messiah Yeshua.
15  Where then is that gratulation of yourselves? for I bear you witness, that, if possible, you all would have plucked out your eyes and given them to me.
16  So then am I become your enemy, by telling you the truth?
17  They zealously seek you in no good way; no, they desire to shut you out, that you all may seek them.
18  But it is good to be zealously sought in a good matter at all times, and not only when I am present with you.
19  My little children, of whom I am again in travail until Messiah be formed in you—
20  but I could wish to be present with you now, and to change my tone; for I am perplexed about you.
---
21  Tell me, you all that desire to be under the Torah, do you all not hear the Torah?
22  For it is written, that Abraham had two sons, one by the handmaid, and one by the freewoman.
23  However the son by the handmaid is born after the flesh; but the son by the freewoman is born through promise.
24  Which things contain an allegory: for these women are two covenants; one from mount Sinai, bearing children to bondage, which is Hagar.
» — Paul labels his own reading *allēgoroumena* ("spoken allegorically"); the Hagar–Sinai–present-Jerusalem against Sarah–promise–Jerusalem-above mapping is a *mashal*, an established rabbinic mode of figural exposition.
25  Now this Hagar is mount Sinai in Arabia and answers to the Jerusalem that now is: for she is in bondage with her children.
26  But the Jerusalem that is above is free, which is our mother.
27  For it is written,
    “Rejoice, you barren that bear not;
    Break forth and cry, you that travail not:
    For more are the children of the desolate than of her that has the husband.”
→ Rejoice, you barren that bear not :: Isaiah 54:1
28  Now we, brothers, as Isaac was, are children of promise.
29  But as then he that was born after the flesh persecuted him that was born after the Spirit, so also it is now.
30  However what says the scripture? “Cast out the handmaid and her son: for the son of the handmaid shall not inherit with the son of the freewoman.”
→ Cast out the handmaid and her son :: Genesis 21:10
31  Therefore, brothers, we are not children of a handmaid, but of the freewoman.

## Galatians 5

1  For freedom did Messiah set us free: stand fast therefore, and be not entangled again in a yoke of bondage.
» *ol* ("yoke") — rabbinic piety prized the yoke of the commandments and the yoke of the kingdom of heaven (m. Berakhot 2:2) as joy; Paul reverses the image, calling the circumcision-law pressed on Gentiles a yoke of bondage.
2  Behold, I Paul say to you, that, if you all receive circumcision, Messiah will profit you nothing.
3  Indeed, I testify again to every man that receives circumcision, that he is a debtor to do the whole Torah.
» — the proselyte who accepts circumcision becomes a *ger tzedek* bound to the whole Torah, every commandment of it; Paul holds the Galatians to that logic to show the true cost of taking on the Torah for justification.
4  You all are severed from Messiah, you all who would be justified by the Torah; you all are fallen away from grace.
5  For we through the Spirit by faith wait for the hope of righteousness.
6  For in Messiah Yeshua neither circumcision avails anything, nor uncircumcision; but faith working through love.
7  You all were running well; who hindered you that you all should not obey the truth?
8  This persuasion came not of Him that calls you.
9  A little leaven leavens the whole lump.
10  I have confidence to you-ward in the Lord, that you all will be none otherwise minded: but he that troubles you shall bear his judgment, whoever he be.
11  But I, brothers, if I still preach circumcision, why am I still persecuted? then has the stumbling-block of the cross been done away.
12  I would that they that unsettle you would even go beyond circumcision.
---
13  For you all, brothers, were called for freedom; only use not your freedom for an occasion to the flesh, but through love be servants one to another.
14  For the whole Torah is fulfilled in one word, even in this: “You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”
→ You shall love your neighbor as yourself :: Leviticus 19:18
15  But if you all bite and devour one another, take heed that you all be not consumed one of another.
---
16  But I say, Walk by the Spirit, and you all shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.
17  For the flesh lusts against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; for these are contrary the one to the other; that you all may not do the things that you all would.
» — flesh warring against Spirit restates the rabbinic anthropology of the two inclinations, *yetzer ha-ra* and *yetzer ha-tov*, contending within a person; Paul locates the victory in walking by the Spirit.
18  But if you all are led by the Spirit, you all are not under the Torah.
19  Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these: fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,[br]
20  idolatry, sorcery, enmities, strife, jealousies, wraths, factions, divisions, parties,[br]
21  envyings, drunkenness, wild parties, and such like; of which I forewarn you, even as I did forewarn you, that they who practise such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.
22  But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faithfulness,[br]
23  meekness, self-control; against such there is no law.
24  And they that are of Messiah Yeshua have crucified the flesh with the passions and the lusts thereof.
25  If we live by the Spirit, by the Spirit let us also walk.
26  Let us not become vainglorious, provoking one another, envying one another.

## Galatians 6

1  Brothers, even if a man be overtaken in any trespass, you all who are spiritual, restore such a one in a spirit of gentleness; looking to yourself, lest you also be tempted.
2  Bear you all one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the Torah of Messiah.
» — "the Torah of Messiah" answers to the rabbinic expectation of *torat ha-mashiach*, the Torah of the Messiah which tradition held he would expound; for Paul it is fulfilled in bearing one another's burdens.
3  For if a man thinks himself to be something when he is nothing, he deceives himself.
4  But let each man prove his own work, and then shall he have his glorying in regard of himself alone, and not of his neighbor.
5  For each man shall bear his own burden.
6  But let him that is taught in the word communicate to him that teaches in all good things.
7  Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatever a man sows, that shall he also reap.
8  For he that sows to his own flesh shall of the flesh reap corruption; but he that sows to the Spirit shall of the Spirit reap eternal life.
9  And let us not be weary in well-doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.
10  So then, as we have opportunity, let us work that which is good toward all men, and especially toward them that are of the household of the faith.
---
11  See with how large letters I write to you with my own hand.
12  As many as desire to make a fair show in the flesh, they compel you to be circumcised; only that they may not be persecuted for the cross of Messiah.
13  For not even they who receive circumcision do themselves keep the Torah; but they desire to have you circumcised, that they may glory in your flesh.
14  But far be it from me to glory, save in the cross of our Lord Yeshua Messiah, through which the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.
15  For neither is circumcision anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creature.
16  And as many as shall walk by this rule, peace be upon them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.
17  Henceforth let no man trouble me; for I bear branded on my body the marks of Yeshua.
» *stigmata* ("marks / brands") — the marks a master burned into a slave to show ownership; scarred from his beatings, Paul bears them as the brand of one who belongs to Yeshua, his master.
18  The grace of our Lord Yeshua Messiah be with your spirit, brothers. Amen.
