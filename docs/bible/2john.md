# 2 John

## 2 John 1

1  The elder to the elect lady and her children, whom I love in truth; and not I only, but also all they that know the truth;
» the elect lady :: *eklektē kyria* — possibly a literal individual, but more likely (as with "your elect sister" in v13) a personification of a local congregation, an epistolary convention also seen in 1 Peter 5:13's "she that is in Babylon, elect together with you."
2  for the truth's sake which abides in us, and it shall be with us for ever: [br]
3  Grace, mercy, peace shall be with us, from God the Father, and from Yeshua Messiah, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.
>>>
4  I rejoice greatly that I have found certain of your children walking in truth, even as we received commandment from the Father.
5  And now I urge you, dear lady, not as though I wrote to you a new commandment, but that which we had from the beginning, that we love one another.
» urge :: *erōtō* — "I ask/urge," the same verb used at John 4:40, 4:47.
6  And this is love, that we should walk after His commandments. [br] This is the commandment, even as you all heard from the beginning, that you all should walk in it.
---
7  For many deceivers are gone forth into the world, even they that confess not that Yeshua Messiah comes in the flesh. This is the deceiver and the antichrist.
→ confess not that Yeshua Messiah comes in the flesh :: 1 John 4:2-3
» the deceiver and the antichrist :: an early docetic denial — that Messiah only seemed to come "in the flesh" — already answered at length in 1 John 4:2-3; the same test of true confession is applied here to a specific local situation.
8  Look to yourselves, that you all lose not the things which we have wrought, but that you all receive a full reward.
9  Whoever goes onward and abides not in the teaching of Messiah, has not God: he that abides in the teaching, the same has both the Father and the Son.
---
10  If any one comes to you, and brings not this teaching, receive him not into your house, and give him no greeting:
» give him no greeting :: in the ancient world a formal greeting carried real social weight — extending it functioned as public endorsement. Withholding it from a false teacher was a concrete act, not mere coldness.
11  for he that gives him greeting partakes in his evil works.
>>>
12  Having many things to write to you, I would not write them with paper and ink: but I hope to come to you, and to speak face to face, that your joy may be made full.
13  The children of your elect sister greet you.
