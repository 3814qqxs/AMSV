# Philemon

## Philemon 1

1  Paul, a prisoner of Messiah Yeshua, [br] and Timothy our brother, [br] to Philemon our beloved and fellow-worker,
2  and to Apphia our sister, [br] and to Archippus our fellow-soldier, [br] and to the assembly in your house:
3  Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Yeshua Messiah.
>>>
4  I thank my God always, [br] making mention of you in my prayers,
5  hearing of your love, [br] and of the faith which you have toward the Lord Yeshua, [br] and toward all the saints;
6  that the fellowship of your faith may become effectual, [br] in the knowledge of every good thing which is in you, to Messiah.
7  For I had much joy and comfort in your love, [br] because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you, brother. [br]
» hearts of the saints :: *ta splanchna* (τὰ σπλάγχνα, "the inward parts/bowels") — the same word recurs at "my very heart" (v12) and "let me have joy of you" (v20, a related wordplay); Paul's appeal for Onesimus runs on this one repeated image of visceral affection.
8  Therefore, though I have all boldness in Messiah to enjoin you that which is befitting,
9  yet for love’s sake I rather appeal, [br] being such a one as Paul the aged, [br] and now a prisoner also of Messiah Yeshua:
» Paul the aged :: *presbytēs* (πρεσβύτης, "old man") — some manuscripts read the nearly identical *presbeutēs* (πρεσβευτής, "ambassador"); either reading fits Paul's strategy of appealing rather than commanding.
---
10  I appeal to you for my child, [br] whom I have begotten in my bonds, [br] Onesimus,
→ Onesimus :: Colossians 4:9
11  who once was unprofitable to you, [br] but now is profitable to you and to me:
» unprofitable to you, but now is profitable to you :: *achrēston*/*euchrēston* (ἄχρηστον/εὔχρηστον) — a pun on Onesimus's own name (*Onēsimos*, "useful, profitable"); Paul says the runaway has finally become what his name always promised.
12  whom I have sent back to you in his own person, [br] that is, my very heart:
13  whom I would gladly have kept with me, [br] that in your behalf he might minister to me in the bonds of the good news:
14  but without your mind I would do nothing; [br] that your goodness should not be as of necessity, but of free will.
15  For perhaps he was therefore parted from you for a season, [br] that you should have him for ever;
16  no longer as a slave, [br] but more than a slave, [br] a brother beloved, specially to me, but how much rather to you, [br] both in the flesh and in the Lord.
→ no longer as a slave, but more than a slave :: Galatians 3:28
>>>
17  If then you count me a partner, [br] receive him as myself.
18  But if he has wronged you at all, or owes you anything, [br] put that to my account;
19  I Paul write it with my own hand, [br] I will repay it: [br] that I say not to you that you owe to me even your own self besides.
» I Paul write it with my own hand :: Paul's autograph guarantees the promise like a signature on a loan document (cf. Galatians 6:11; 2 Thessalonians 3:17) — vv18-19 read like a formal promissory note inserted into the letter.
20  Yes, brother, let me have joy of you in the Lord: [br] refresh my heart in Messiah.
» let me have joy of you :: *onaimēn* (ὀναίμην, "may I be profited/benefited") — a second wordplay on Onesimus's name, echoing v11: Paul asks Philemon to let Onesimus be, once more, exactly what his name means.
---
21  Having confidence in your obedience I write to you, [br] knowing that you will do even beyond what I say. [br]
22  But prepare me also a lodging: [br] for I hope that through your prayers I shall be granted to you.
>>>
23  Epaphras, my fellow-prisoner in Messiah Yeshua, greets you;
24  and so do Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke, my fellow-workers.
→ Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke :: Colossians 4:10-14
25  The grace of our Lord Yeshua Messiah be with your spirit. Amen.
