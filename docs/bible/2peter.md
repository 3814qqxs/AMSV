# 2 Peter

## 2 Peter 1

1  Simon Peter, a servant and apostle of Yeshua Messiah, to them that have obtained a like precious faith with us in the righteousness of our God and the Saviour Yeshua Messiah:
» Simon Peter :: the Greek reads *Symeōn Petros* (Συμεὼν Πέτρος) — the Hebraized "Symeon," used elsewhere only in James's speech at Acts 15:14, rather than the Hellenized "Simon." Same Kepha/Petros wordplay noted at 1 Peter 1:1.
» the righteousness of our God and the Saviour Yeshua Messiah :: one Greek article governs both "God" and "Saviour" (*tou theou hēmōn kai sōtēros Iēsou Christou*) — a single-referent construction (the same pattern as Titus 2:13), naming Yeshua Messiah directly "our God."
2  Grace to you and peace be multiplied in the knowledge of God and of Yeshua our Lord;
>>>
3  seeing that His divine power has granted to us all things that pertain to life and godliness, through the knowledge of Him that called us by His own glory and virtue;
4  by which He has granted to us His precious and exceeding great promises; that through these you all may become partakers of the divine nature, having escaped from the corruption that is in the world by lust.
» partakers of the divine nature :: *theias physeōs* — the boldest "divine nature" language anywhere in the NT, unique to this verse.
>>>
5  Indeed, and for this very cause adding on your part all diligence, [br] in your faith supply virtue; [br] and in your virtue knowledge;
6  and in your knowledge self-control; [br] and in your self-control patience; [br] and in your patience godliness;
7  and in your godliness brotherly kindness; [br] and in your brotherly kindness love.
» a *sorites* (climax chain) — a recognized Greco-Roman rhetorical form, each term picked up as the next clause's starting point, also used by Stoic ethical writers. The chain's final rung is love, the same summit Paul's own list reaches (1 Corinthians 13).
>>>
8  For if these things are yours and abound, they make you to be not idle nor unfruitful to the knowledge of our Lord Yeshua Messiah.
9  For he that lacks these things is blind, seeing only what is near, having forgotten the cleansing from his old sins.
10  Therefore, brothers, give the more diligence to make your calling and election sure: for if you all do these things, you all shall never stumble:
11  for thus shall be richly supplied to you the entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Yeshua Messiah.
>>>
12  Therefore I shall be ready always to put you in remembrance of these things, though you all know them, and are established in the truth which is with you.
13  And I think it right, as long as I am in this tabernacle, to stir you up by putting you in remembrance;
» this tabernacle :: *skēnōmati*, "tent" — a body-as-tent metaphor, and a pointed word choice: Peter once offered to build three *skēnas* ("tabernacles") for Yeshua, Moses, and Elijah at the Transfiguration (Matthew 17:4) — the very scene he recalls two verses later (vv16-18).
14  knowing that the putting off of my tabernacle comes swiftly, even as our Lord Yeshua Messiah signified to me.
→ knowing that the putting off of my tabernacle comes swiftly, even as our Lord Yeshua Messiah signified to me :: John 21:18-19
15  Indeed, I will give diligence that at every time you all may be able after my decease to call these things to remembrance.
>>>
16  For we did not follow cunningly devised fables, when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Yeshua Messiah, but we were eyewitnesses of His majesty.
→ we were eyewitnesses of His majesty :: Matthew 17:1-5
17  For He received from God the Father honor and glory, when there was borne such a voice to Him by the Majestic Glory, “This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased”:
18  and this voice we ourselves heard borne out of heaven, when we were with Him in the holy mount.
>>>
19  And we have the word of prophecy made more sure; to which you all do well that you all take heed, as to a lamp shining in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day-star arise in your hearts:
→ the day-star arise in your hearts :: Revelation 22:16
» day-star :: *phōsphoros*, "light-bringer" — the same title Messiah claims for Himself, "the bright, the morning star" (Revelation 22:16), itself drawn from Balaam's oracle, "a star shall come forth out of Jacob" (Numbers 24:17).
20  knowing this first, that no prophecy of scripture is of private interpretation.
» of private interpretation :: *idias epilyseōs* ("of one's own release/interpretation") — ambiguous between "no prophecy originates from the prophet's own interpreting" (favored by v21's follow-through, "no prophecy ever came by the will of man") and "no one may privately interpret prophecy." The verse that follows argues for the first.
21  For no prophecy ever came by the will of man: but men spoke from God, being moved by the Holy Spirit.
» men spoke from God, being moved by the Holy Spirit :: the same account of prophetic inspiration as 1 Peter 1:10-11, where "the Spirit of Messiah" already at work in the prophets searched their own oracles.

## 2 Peter 2

1  But there arose false prophets also among the people, as among you also there shall be false teachers, who shall privily bring in destructive heresies, denying even the Master that bought them, bringing upon themselves swift destruction.
» this chapter runs closely parallel to Jude 4-13, sharing images and structure in an order too close to be independent — the two letters are literarily related, though the direction of dependence (or a shared source) is debated.
» the Master that bought them :: *despotēs* ("Master/absolute owner," stronger than the ordinary *kyrios*) paired with "bought" (*agorasantos*) — the same redemption-by-purchase image as 1 Peter 1:18-19, "redeemed... with precious blood."
2  And many shall follow their lascivious doings; by reason of whom the way of the truth shall be evil spoken of.
3  And in covetousness shall they with feigned words make merchandise of you: whose sentence now from of old lingers not, and their destruction slumbers not.
>>>
4  For if God spared not angels when they sinned, [br] but cast them down to hell, and committed them to pits of darkness, to be reserved to judgment;
→ cast them down to hell, and committed them to pits of darkness :: Jude 6
» cast them down to hell :: *tartarōsas* ("cast into Tartarus") — a NT hapax, borrowing the Greek mythological name for the deepest abyss (Hesiod, *Theogony*). Parallels 1 Peter 3:19-20's "spirits in prison" and Jude 6's "everlasting bonds," all drawing on the Second Temple Enoch tradition of imprisoned fallen angels before the flood.
5  and spared not the ancient world, [br] but preserved Noah with seven others, a preacher of righteousness, when he brought a flood upon the world of the ungodly;
6  and turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah into ashes condemned them with an overthrow, [br] having made them an example to those that should live ungodly;
→ turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah into ashes :: Genesis 19:24-25
7  and delivered righteous Lot, greatly distressed by the lascivious life of the wicked
8  (for that righteous man dwelling among them, in seeing and hearing, vexed his righteous soul from day to day with their lawless deeds):
9  the Lord knows how to deliver the godly out of temptation, and to keep the unrighteous under punishment to the day of judgment;
>>>
10  but chiefly them that walk after the flesh in the lust of defilement, and despise dominion. [br] Daring, self-willed, they tremble not to rail at dignities:
11  whereas angels, though greater in might and power, bring not a railing judgment against them before the Lord.
» bring not a railing judgment :: Jude's version of this same statement (Jude 9) names the specific incident — Michael the archangel disputing with the devil over Moses' body — that 2 Peter leaves general.
12  But these, as creatures without reason, born mere animals to be taken and destroyed, railing in matters of which they are ignorant, shall in their destroying surely be destroyed,
13  suffering wrong as the hire of wrong-doing; [br] men that count it pleasure to revel in the day-time, [br] spots and blemishes, [br] revelling in their deceivings while they feast with you;
14  having eyes full of adultery, and that cannot cease from sin; [br] enticing unsteadfast souls; [br] having a heart exercised in covetousness; [br] children of cursing;
» children of cursing :: a Semitic genitive ("sons/children of X" = characterized by X) — the same construction behind "son of His love" (Colossians 1:13) and "children of obedience" (1 Peter 1:14).
15  forsaking the right way, they went astray, having followed the way of Balaam the son of Beor, who loved the hire of wrong-doing;
→ having followed the way of Balaam the son of Beor :: Numbers 22:21-35; 31:16
16  but he was rebuked for his own transgression: a dumb ass spoke with man’s voice and stayed the madness of the prophet.
>>>
17  These are springs without water, and mists driven by a storm; for whom the blackness of darkness has been reserved.
» springs without water, and mists driven by a storm :: Jude's parallel (Jude 12-13) runs longer — waterless clouds, fruitless autumn trees, wild sea-waves, wandering stars — the same catalogue of empty, unstable images.
18  For, uttering great swelling words of vanity, they entice in the lusts of the flesh, by lasciviousness, those who are just escaping from them that live in error;
19  promising them liberty, while they themselves are bondservants of corruption; for of whom a man is overcome, of the same is he also brought into bondage.
20  For if, after they have escaped the defilements of the world through the knowledge of the Lord and Saviour Yeshua Messiah, they are again entangled therein and overcome, the last state is become worse with them than the first.
21  For it were better for them not to have known the way of righteousness, than, after knowing it, to turn back from the holy commandment delivered to them.
22  It has happened to them according to the true proverb, [br] “The dog turning to his own vomit again, [br] and the sow that had washed to wallowing in the mire.”
→ The dog turning to his own vomit again :: Proverbs 26:11
» the sow that had washed to wallowing in the mire :: not part of the Proverbs citation — the first half quotes Proverbs 26:11 directly; this second half is likely a known folk-proverb Peter adds alongside it.

## 2 Peter 3

1  This is now, beloved, the second epistle that I write to you; and in both of them I stir up your sincere mind by putting you in remembrance;
» the second epistle :: confirms common authorship with 1 Peter, addressed, it implies, to a shared or overlapping readership.
2  that you all should remember the words which were spoken before by the holy prophets, and the commandment of the Lord and Saviour through your apostles:
>>>
3  knowing this first, that in the last days mockers shall come with mockery, walking after their own lusts,
4  and saying, “Where is the promise of His coming? for, from the day that the fathers fell asleep, all things continue as they were from the beginning of the creation.”
5  For this they wilfully forget, that there were heavens from of old, and an earth compacted out of water and amidst water, by the word of God;
6  by which means the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished:
7  but the heavens that now are, and the earth, by the same word have been stored up for fire, being reserved against the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.
→ stored up for fire, being reserved against the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men :: Malachi 4:1
>>>
8  But forget not this one thing, beloved, that one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.
→ one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day :: Psalm 90:4
9  The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, as some count slackness; but is longsuffering to you-ward, not wishing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.
10  But the day of the Lord will come as a thief; in the which the heavens shall pass away with a great noise, and the elements shall be dissolved with fervent heat, and the earth and the works that are therein shall be burned up.
→ the day of the Lord will come as a thief :: 1 Thessalonians 5:2
» as a thief :: the same image Yeshua used of His own return (Matthew 24:43), which Paul had already put in writing to the Thessalonians (1 Thessalonians 5:2) — a shared apostolic figure of speech, notable given vv15-16 below.
>>>
11  Seeing that these things are thus all to be dissolved, what manner of persons ought you all to be in all holy living and godliness,
12  looking for and earnestly desiring the coming of the day of God, by reason of which the heavens being on fire shall be dissolved, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat?
13  But, according to His promise, we look for new heavens and a new earth, in which dwells righteousness.
→ we look for new heavens and a new earth :: Isaiah 65:17
14  Therefore, beloved, seeing that you all look for these things, give diligence that you all may be found in peace, without spot and blameless in His sight.
>>>
15  And account that the longsuffering of our Lord is salvation; even as our beloved brother Paul also, according to the wisdom given to him, wrote to you;
» our beloved brother Paul :: one apostle explicitly commending another's writings — a rare direct cross-endorsement within the NT itself.
16  as also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things; in which are some things hard to be understood, which the ignorant and unsteadfast wrest, as they do also the other scriptures, to their own destruction.
» the other scriptures :: Peter calls Paul's letters *graphas* ("scriptures") — the same word used for the Tanakh in the same breath ("the other scriptures"). One of the clearest windows the NT gives into its own writers already sensing apostolic letters as scripture alongside the Hebrew canon.
17  You all therefore, beloved, knowing these things beforehand, beware lest, being carried away with the error of the wicked, you all fall from your own steadfastness.
18  But grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Yeshua Messiah. To Him be the glory both now and for ever. Amen.
» To Him be the glory :: a closing doxology directed to Messiah by name, not only to the Father — closing the letter the way it opened (1:1, "our God and Saviour Yeshua Messiah").
