## D. Reviewed — no change proposed

- **Policy — archaic word order, not proposed:** "therefore has the curse been poured out" (9:11), "yet have we not entreated" (9:13), "Therefore has YHWH watched" (9:14), "for under the whole heaven has not been done" (9:12), "defer not" is the only imperative changed (9:19). Same as the Matthew caches.
- 9:2 "understood by the books" (*binoti ba-sefarim*): retained.
- 9:3 "the Lord God": *Adonai ha-Elohim*; retained. "Oh, Lord" (*anna Adonai*): retained.
- 9:3–19 divine names: the Lord/YHWH alternation follows the Hebrew (*Adonai* vs *YHWH*); no change. 9:17 "for the Lord's sake": retained.
- 9:5 "ordinances" (*mishpatim*): retained.
- 9:12 "judges" (*shofetim*): retained.
- 9:13 "entreated the favor" (*chillinu et penei*): retained.
- 9:21 "being caused to fly swiftly" (*mu'af bi'af*): obscure phrase; retained.
- 9:23 "consider the matter": retained.
- 9:25 "street and moat" (*rechov ve-charuts*): retained; *charuts* is uncertain.
- 9:26 "desolations are determined": retained.
- Interpretive questions (the sevens' start and end points, the identity of the prince and of the anointed one) are not annotated beyond scriptural cross-references.
- R8 lineation: none; the prayer is prose.
- R9: the prayer (4–19) and Gabriel's reply (22–27) are each one layer; no nested quotation.
