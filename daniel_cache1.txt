## D. Reviewed — no change proposed

- **Policy — archaic word order, not proposed:** "seed royal" (1:3), "therefore stood they" (1:19), "came Nebuchadnezzar" (1:1). Same as the Matthew caches.
- 1:1 "third year": Jeremiah 25:1 and 46:2 date Nebuchadnezzar's first regnal year to Jehoiakim's fourth. A reckoning (harmonization) question, not philology; no » note proposed.
- 1:2 "the Lord" (*Adonai*): kept; not YHWH. "the house of God" (*beit ha-Elohim*): kept.
- 1:11 "steward" (*melzar*): Akkadian-derived title; kept.
- 1:13, 1:15 "countenance(s)" (*mar'eh*, "appearance"): living formal word; kept (cf. 00025 formal-register precedent).
- 1:9 "God made Daniel to find": idiomatic *vayyiten ha-Elohim*; intelligible; kept.
- 1:21 "continued": Hebrew *vayehi* ("was"); intelligible; kept.
- R9: speech balance — 1:10 (the chief's reply, one layer), 1:12–13 (Daniel, one layer). v.3 and v.8 are reported speech: not quoted.
- R8 lineation: none in ch.1.

## E. Full proposed chapter text (Daniel 1)

## Daniel 1

---
1  In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah came Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to Jerusalem, and besieged it.
→ third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah :: 2 Kings 24:1; 2 Chronicles 36:6–7; Jeremiah 25:1; Jeremiah 46:2
2  And the Lord gave Jehoiakim king of Judah into his hand, with part of the vessels of the house of God; and he carried them into the land of Shinar to the house of his god: and he brought the vessels into the treasure-house of his god.
→ the Lord gave Jehoiakim king of Judah into his hand :: 2 Kings 24:1–2; Jeremiah 25:9; Jeremiah 27:6
→ vessels of the house of God :: 2 Kings 24:13; 2 Chronicles 36:7; 2 Chronicles 36:18; Jeremiah 27:19–22; Ezra 1:7; Daniel 5:2
→ land of Shinar :: Genesis 10:10; Genesis 11:2; Zechariah 5:11
» the Lord gave :: *Adonai* ("Lord," sovereign master) — the Tetragrammaton does not appear in Daniel until 9:2.
3  And the king spoke to Ashpenaz the master of his eunuchs, that he should bring in certain of the children of Israel, even of the seed royal and of the nobles;
→ even of the seed royal and of the nobles :: 2 Kings 20:17–18; Isaiah 39:6–7; 2 Kings 24:12–15
4  youths in whom was no blemish, but well-favored,[br] and skillful in all wisdom, and endowed with knowledge, and discerning in learning,[br] and such as had ability to stand in the king’s palace;[br] and that he should teach them the literature and the tongue of the Chaldeans.
→ well-favored :: Genesis 39:6
→ ability to stand in the king’s palace :: Genesis 41:46; 1 Kings 10:8
→ the tongue of the Chaldeans :: Genesis 11:28; Genesis 11:31; 2 Kings 24:2
» the Chaldeans :: *Kasdim* ("Chaldeans") — here the people and their language; in 2:2, 2:4 the same word names a learned class.
5  And the king appointed for them a daily portion of the king’s delicacies, and of the wine which he drank, and that they should be nourished three years; that at the end of that time they should stand before the king.
6  Now among these were, of the children of Judah, Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.
» Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah :: *Daniyyel* ("God is my judge"), *Chananyah* ("YHWH has been gracious"), *Mishael* ("who is what God is?"), *Azaryah* ("YHWH has helped") — each name carries *El* or *Yah*.
→ Daniel :: Ezekiel 14:14; Ezekiel 14:20; Ezekiel 28:3
7  And the chief of the eunuchs gave names to them:[br] to Daniel he gave the name of Belteshazzar;[br] and to Hananiah, of Shadrach;[br] and to Mishael, of Meshach;[br] and to Azariah, of Abed-nego.
→ he gave the name of Belteshazzar :: Genesis 41:45; 2 Kings 23:34; 2 Kings 24:17
» Belteshazzar :: *Belteshatstsar* — "Bel, protect his life" (Akkadian); 4:8 states it was "according to the name of my god." *Abed-nego* = "servant of Nego," likely Nabu. The new names replace the *El*/*Yah* names of v.6.
---
8  But Daniel purposed in his heart that he would not defile himself with the king’s delicacies, nor with the wine which he drank: therefore he requested of the chief of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself.
→ defile himself with the king’s delicacies :: Ezekiel 4:13–14; Hosea 9:3–4; Leviticus 11:1–47; Leviticus 20:25–26
» defile himself :: *yitga'al* (root *g-'-l*, "to be soiled, polluted") — not *tame'*, the usual term for ritual uncleanness; cf. Isaiah 59:3; Malachi 1:7, 1:12.
9  Now God made Daniel to find kindness and compassion in the sight of the chief of the eunuchs.
→ kindness and compassion :: Genesis 39:21; 1 Kings 8:50; Nehemiah 1:11; Zechariah 7:9
10  And the chief of the eunuchs said to Daniel, “I fear my lord the king, who has appointed your food and your drink: for why should he see your faces more haggard than the youths who are of your own age? so would you all endanger my head with the king.”[br]
11  Then said Daniel to the steward whom the chief of the eunuchs had appointed over Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah:
12  “Test your servants, please, ten days; and let them give us vegetables to eat, and water to drink.[br]
→ vegetables to eat :: Genesis 1:29
13  Then let our countenances be looked upon before you, and the countenance of the youths who eat of the king’s delicacies; and as you see, deal with your servants.”[br]
14  So he listened to them in this matter, and tested them ten days.
15  And at the end of ten days their countenances appeared fairer, and they were fatter in flesh, than all the youths who did eat of the king’s delicacies.
→ fatter in flesh :: Genesis 41:2; Genesis 41:18
16  So the steward took away their delicacies, and the wine that they should drink, and gave them vegetables.
17  Now as for these four youths, God gave them knowledge and skill in all literature and wisdom: and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams.
→ God gave them knowledge and skill :: Exodus 31:3; Exodus 35:31; 1 Kings 3:12
→ understanding in all visions and dreams :: Genesis 40:8; Genesis 41:15–16; Numbers 12:6
---
18  And at the end of the days which the king had appointed for bringing them in, the chief of the eunuchs brought them in before Nebuchadnezzar.
19  And the king spoke with them; and among them all was found none like Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah: therefore stood they before the king.
20  And in every matter of wisdom and understanding, concerning which the king inquired of them, he found them ten times better than all the magicians and enchanters who were in all his realm.
→ magicians and enchanters :: Genesis 41:8; Exodus 7:11; Exodus 7:22
21  And Daniel continued even to the first year of king Cyrus.
→ the first year of king Cyrus :: 2 Chronicles 36:22–23; Ezra 1:1–4; Isaiah 44:28; Isaiah 45:1; Daniel 10:1
